# Period-specific construction studies

For every branch name a place, bounded date, use context and fidelity goal first. Build a feature ledger linking roof, wall assembly, fasteners, tools, textiles and markings to supplied/verified evidence. The following are modeling procedures and research questions, not declarations that one motif represents an entire culture. Unsourced dimensions/materials remain proposals. Keep an inferred alternative separate from a documented reconstruction.

## Western frontier and settled town

Choose actual settlement, decade and building use before selecting a cinematic false-front vocabulary. Build one frontage as wall structure, cladding, roof/back side, doorway and walkway; include a service/delivery side rather than a facade-only stage. Add a dated daily prop and horse/cart/person proxy only where the brief requires them. Check board/beam dimensions and fasteners against sources, preserving repeated-bay connection planes.

Use exposure-dependent dust/paint wear and hand-contact polish, not universal orange grading. Inspect neutral daylight, street-eye and rear construction views. A miniature appearance needs timber/tool scale repair; an unsupported veranda needs actual supports/joinery. Deliver evidence ledger, frontage/back assembly and clean/used states; genre familiarity is not historical proof.

## Pirate and maritime port

Specify region, ship/port use and date rather than assembling every skull, cannon and sail motif. Build a dock slice with tide/water level, piles, decking, mooring attachment and cargo path. For a ship component define deck curvature, rail and rigging attachment from chosen evidence; rope must reach belaying/anchoring points and respect gravity. Keep working access around hatches and lines.

Differentiate wet splash-zone wood, drier sheltered surfaces, rope and metal corrosion. Test low dock-eye and vessel-eye views plus an attachment close-up. If cargo blocks the entire working path, revise staging; floating ropes need endpoint/tension correction. Deliver waterline/tide assumptions and separate dock/rope/cargo collections. Sailing feasibility and marine engineering remain outside art inspection.

## Japanese warrior-era setting and infiltration fiction

Choose locality/date/social setting and distinguish researched architecture from later entertainment shorthand. Build a gate/room bay with documented structural rhythm, floor transition, roof edge and movable screen/door mechanism. Use a human proxy to test opening/threshold and equipment display clearance. If a ninja/infiltration route is requested, add only approved vents/ledges/access hardware consistent with the chosen construction or explicitly label alternate history.

Keep timber, plaster/paper/roof finish and metal fittings distinct according to references. Inspect neutral front/side and open/closed states before dramatic moonlight. Generic mixed-century armor or motifs need replacement or a declared fictional blend, not a widened date label. Deliver construction/state sheet and feature sources; Blender shadows do not validate stealth.

## Viking-age or other Scandinavian period

Do not infer one universal hall/ship/costume from the broad label. Select settlement/use/date and build a structural bay, roof support, hearth/work area and doorway from references. Keep occupation-scale objects and tool storage accessible. If a maritime element is included, document its own source rather than transferring every building ornament onto a hull.

Use material layering and localized soot/wear tied to the selected ventilation/hearth arrangement. A bright neutral cutaway should expose support and circulation; atmospheric firelight is an additional view. If carved detail overwhelms documented plain structure, reduce it. Deliver roof/wall junction, everyday prop and evidence exceptions; mythology and fantasy belong to explicitly named variants.

## Ancient Egypt

Choose period, locality and domestic/administrative/ceremonial function; monumental stone, ordinary buildings and interiors need different evidence. Construct wall core/finish, opening/lintel and a repeated bay with a human-scale insert. Add a source-backed furnishing/container rather than coating every object in gold. Keep any undeciphered/unsourced inscription as a clearly labeled neutral placeholder shape, not fabricated authentic text.

Separate stone/finish, textiles and metal/leaf layers. Compare ungraded sun/shade and construction section; museum aging is not necessarily use-time appearance. If the model is an undifferentiated sand-colored block, restore sourced surface/structural divisions. Deliver feature ledger and proposed versus supported polychromy variants without declaring authenticity from rendering quality.

## Ancient Greece

Specify date/place/building type and a particular source for proportions/details. Build base, support, beam/roof junction and wall/enclosure as separate elements; repeat the bay through a corner before adding ornament. Do not combine different orders/details by convenience unless the brief names an invented hybrid. Include everyday scale and access, not only a temple hero shot.

Treat weathered museum marble and reconstructed painted/finished surfaces as different evidence states. Use neutral side light to inspect profile continuity and seams. If a repeated column/capital changes scale at corners, repair shared module dimensions. Deliver profile/source sheet, junction and finish assumptions; proportional plausibility is not archaeological verification.

## Ancient Rome

Choose date/place/function and research wall core, facing, roof/vault and service details independently. Model a bay section that explains structure beneath decorative facing, with doorway/drainage and human use. Mosaic/floor panels need actual region/scale and gutters; do not randomly mix symbols from unrelated sites. Build a broken/cutaway derivative without destroying the intact master.

Separate core, plaster/stone facing, metal and floor finish. Inspect neutral cutaway, floor grazing angle and entry view; a shiny marble coating alone cannot establish the setting. Floating facades need connection/edge repair. Deliver supported versus reconstructed finish states and ledger; do not imply that a decorative arch model proves structural stability.

## Victorian and industrial urban settings

Select city, decade, class/use and technology before choosing brick, iron, wallpaper or glass. Build one repeated facade/interior bay with floor structure, opening, drainage and service access. A machinery insert needs power/transmission and operator clearance at the chosen scale. Keep mass-produced versus handmade details internally consistent with evidence.

Use distinct brick/mortar, painted wood, metal and paper finishes with wear at use/exposure. Test daylight window response and practical-lit interior separately. Mixed modern fittings require verification or removal. Deliver facade/corner/service junction and dated furnishing sheet; aesthetic industrial richness alone does not establish period accuracy.

## Modern civilian setting

Bound the decade/locality and building function; modern is not a single worldwide kit. Construct facade/enclosure, access/service panels and a daily-use object with documented dimensions or explicit design proposals. Keep accessibility/code claims separate unless actual requirements are supplied and checked. Show power/communication/maintenance routing only where the brief needs it.

Use finish/manufacturing consistency, restrained dirt and legible product-state variants. Inspect human-eye, service-open and neutral material views. If all equipment differs in manufacturing era, reconcile the procurement/history rather than applying uniform wear. Deliver date/source/assumption ledger and state kit; code compliance and equipment function remain unverified without their own tests.

## World War I-era military kit

Name year, theater, side/unit and role; verify clothing/webbing, helmet, tools, transport and markings as separate evidence items. Build a mannequin/loadout or emplacement slice from those sources with actual attachment and reachable storage. Avoid copying later optics/rails or treating every vehicle as interchangeable. Preserve a neutral unmarked variant until markings are verified.

Separate canvas/leather/metal/paint according to the chosen kit and place wear by handling/environment. Inspect silhouette, attachment close-up and equipment-overlap poses. Unsupported pieces need replacement or an alternate-history label. Deliver the dated inventory and uncertainties; asset inspection is neither equipment-function nor tactical advice/testing.

## World War II-era military kit

Repeat the year/theater/unit/role discipline rather than reusing a generic earlier kit. Establish which production variant and service state the references show. Build mounting brackets, stowage and crew access for vehicle/equipment sets; keep repeated parts at consistent manufacturing scale. Match camouflage/markings to the selected evidence and preserve an unmarked master.

Review neutral paint/cloth/metal response, profile and occupied/service-open views. A correctly shaped shell with incompatible attachments still fails the dated contract. Repair the specific mismatch rather than adding battle damage to obscure it. Deliver component/date ledger and crew-clearance study; no operational or historical-authenticity conclusion without adequate sources.

## Cold War military and civilian contrast

Choose a bounded decade, locality, organization and role; technology can coexist across ages through service history, but document that rather than guessing. Build one control/workspace or vehicle-service module with period interface layout, mounting and cable paths from supplied sources. Analog/digital display and recording equipment need source-backed combinations.

Use coating, plastic/rubber, metal and textile finishes appropriate to the selected objects; avoid making all older equipment rusty. Test screen-off and screen-on with readable controls and neutral scale views. Anachronistic modern connectors require evidence, replacement or explicit fictional retrofit. Deliver base/retrofit variants with the retrofit boundary identified; neither a working-looking control panel nor its labels establish functionality.
