# Liminal place construction branches

Choose the building function and the exact sort of absence before making repetition. Repeated space can be ordinary architecture or a declared camera illusion. Keep module dimensions and texture scale stable; inconsistent joins are defects unless the brief intentionally uses them. Do not add monsters or traumatic history as defaults.

## Backrooms-like interior

Build a wall bay, internal/external corner, ceiling panel, fluorescent fixture and carpet tile around one shared grid. Assemble a compact branching room first; inspect doorway and ceiling joins from visitor height before extending. A mono-yellow palette is relevant only to this selected variant and is not a rule for malls or pools.

Use wallpaper with meaningful seam direction, low-pile carpet response and fixture diffusers separate from illumination. Darkened carpet patches need a placement history rather than uniform wet gloss. Set a destination pool or lateral interruption so the image has depth without turning every boundary black. Repetition can continue beyond view, but reveal only the bounded constructed region you can actually verify.

If it looks like a ruin, reduce random rubble/damage and restore functional lights. If copy-paste seams reveal the grid, repair module boundaries and vary only nonstructural detail. Review a plan with camera limits, visitor shot, carpet/fixture crop and an ungraded image retaining wall/door recognition.

## Empty mall

Construct one retail threshold, shutter/storefront, public floor band, vertical circulation cue and atrium or concourse edge. Repeat the shop bay while keeping a few distinct anchors such as an escalator silhouette or information point. A blank hallway with yellow paint is insufficient mall construction.

Separate polished public floor from shop finishes and painted/metal rail systems. Control broad reflection shapes so the floor remains readable; uniform mirror coating can erase boundaries. Use working overhead/clerestory light with dark or closed shops if the chosen after-hours state calls for it. Compose at visitor height with a visible continuation and plausible guard/rail edges.

If it becomes an airport or office accidentally, restore the selected retail threshold vocabulary. If empty floor has no scale, add dimensioned thresholds/railings before clutter. Accept with concourse/atrium shot, storefront repeat/seam crop and scale/context view. Architecture depiction is not code-compliance certification.

## Empty school

Build classroom-door, corridor-wall, floor transition, fixture and signage/locker modules appropriate to the supplied location/era. Keep ceiling and doorway scale plausible and shared across repeated bays. Lockers are not mandatory where the actual reference uses another system. Use original or supplied notice graphics and avoid inventing real institution claims.

Establish a long-view rhythm and one turn or open classroom threshold. Working fluorescents or daylight can support absence more effectively than universal broken bulbs. Give linoleum/vinyl/tile and painted doors distinct broad finishes, with restrained wear at actual contacts.

If every door differs in height, repair the grid; if it reads as a hospital, revise corridor fixtures and classroom context rather than tinting it. Review corridor approach, classroom reverse view, floor/threshold joins and a bright diagnostic. Accept recognizable educational use and intentional emptiness without claiming a real school's dimensions.

## Poolrooms

Model basin depth, waterline, coping, tiled wall/floor and drainage separately. Do not treat the pool as an infinitely thin blue plane. Match tile size and grout across corners; preserve a scale reference and the basin underside when visible. Water surface geometry and wall interfaces need enough thickness/separation to avoid z-fighting and black transmission artifacts.

Use dielectric tile and water responses with controlled reflection/refraction. Review normal, thickness and overlap before changing emission. Motivate light from actual openings or fixtures; caustic-like presentation must not hide the basin edge. Compare water-on and water-off views at the same camera.

If water becomes black, inspect normals, thickness, paths and renderer support. If reflections swallow the coping, reshape light/environment and viewpoint. Accept with basin section, tile seam crop, water-off/on pair and visitor-height image. A render does not establish swimming, buoyancy or physically exact water behavior.

## Hotel circulation

Build one guest-room doorway, corridor segment, room-number/sign family and an elevator/stair landing. Repetition is organized around plausible circulation nodes; do not stretch one door to fill every gap. Keep a stable carpet pattern orientation and quiet wall finishes supporting the repeated light pools.

Choose a landing or turn as a remembered anchor and frame doors obliquely enough to reveal depth. Mirrors require careful reverse geometry and camera checks; they are optional. If it looks like a residence or office, strengthen hotel-specific entry/wayfinding relationships before adding decorative lamps. Review corridor/landing pair, door scale and reflection diagnostics. Depiction of access does not prove traversable navigation.

## Office after hours

Use a ceiling/light grid, partition/door system, work-zone or meeting-room cue and service/circulation edge. Differentiate public circulation from desk fields even when no people are present. Original signage and a declared use state are preferable to arbitrary ominous slogans.

Test one bay with ceiling, floor and partition seams before repetition. Working lights, glazing reflections and a few retained task objects establish a maintained place. If the brief asks for empty rather than abandoned, avoid scattered papers and broken furniture. Review corridor and meeting-room views plus a grid/seam crop; accept office-specific function, stable scale and a controlled compositional interruption.

## Shared acceptance

For the selected branch deliver editable modules, pivots/grid/material scales, camera limits and actual reviewed visitor/layout/close views. If extending by forced perspective or aligned repeated sets, provide a construction diagram and test only the declared camera range. A convincing still does not prove endless architecture, moving-camera continuity or target-engine behavior.
