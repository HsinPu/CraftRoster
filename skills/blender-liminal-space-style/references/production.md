# Circulation without activity

## Choose building and state

Specify an after-hours mall, school passage, hotel landing or pool corridor. Retain functional clues: drainage, signs, thresholds and fixtures. Decide whether repetition is plausible or deliberately impossible; do not accidentally stretch each module.

## Modular recipe

Build a wall bay, floor segment, fixture and doorway on consistent snap dimensions. Assemble a short passage and turning/branching view, then extend camera-visible regions. Break expectation with one displaced landmark. Yellow carpet suits some Backrooms briefs; poolrooms need tile scale, coping and controlled wet reflections; malls need retail thresholds and atrium rhythm. Do not substitute one palette for all variants.

Use operational-looking lights with broad pools. A 2.6 m school ceiling is a study starting point, subordinate to supplied dimensions. Keep texture scale and fixture spacing consistent. Place a visitor-height camera with controlled verticals and a slightly brighter destination beyond a turn. Atmosphere is optional and must not erase ordinary recognition. Grade mildly.

## Repairs and review

A ruin needs debris removed and active fixtures restored. Obvious copy-paste needs minor wear variation while retaining construction dimensions and a meaningful compositional interruption. A blank place needs functional clues before clutter.

Require visitor view, continuation/layout view and module-seam crop. Accept recognizable building function, believable continuation, consistent tile scale and an intentionally empty mood. Monsters are not needed to establish liminality.
