# Table, party, idle, rhythm and education workflows

## Card game and deck-builder table

Obtain card aspect, minimum card size, stack/hand limits and approved interface masks. Build face/back/edge proxies with thickness and a table layout for hand, draw, discard and play areas. Test maximum overlapping hand/stack arrangement and a raised/selected card state; do not invent new rules. Keep table ornaments outside reserved screen-space areas and retain face UV orientation during flips.

Use quiet felt/wood/neutral surface with controlled highlights and a border/value hierarchy supporting card edges. Rare-pull effects are separate from readable card art. If oblique cards lose borders, repair thickness/edge/lighting rather than sharpening the entire scene. If glow destroys identity, reduce its footprint and preserve a clean card pass. Deliver table/zone map, overlap/state sheet and target-size renders; card text/art rights and gameplay remain separate.

## Simultaneous party stage

Collect participant count, camera crops and personal/shared zones. Build distinct participant stations using both shape and color, plus central action area and state props. Place all actor/token proxies at once with the largest supplied animation/extents. Keep stage-specific objects from covering another participant's region during transitions.

Test wide, split-screen or alternate crops actually requested, in grayscale and reduced size. If colored stations merge when desaturated, change mass/position/icon geometry. If celebration effects obscure others, localize/bound them rather than shrinking the approved player zones. Deliver participant/central-state and maximum-overlap views. Input, scoring and social readability in live play need actual product testing.

## Idle and incremental scene

Define the visual progress axis and actual states: empty/working/full/upgraded, not arbitrary new production rules. Build a station with shared anchors through levels and a small process animation whose motion/emission returns cleanly. Expanded upgrades need stated footprint/overhang, and background activity should not continuously compete with the primary resource/task.

Use stable broad material groups and reserve motion/brightness for state changes. Review a long loop, return seam and a static screenshot at minimum size. If progress is only a color change, add an approved shape/material-state cue. If the loop jerks, fix all animated transforms/material values and velocities, not only first/last pose. Deliver state lineup, loop timing and bounds; low geometry does not prove low runtime cost or economic behavior.

## Rhythm lane and beat stage

Obtain beat/event timing, lane positions, hit-plane and interface exclusions from the actual project. Build lane guides, target frame and background beat props on separate layers. Place visual events at their supplied timestamps and preserve constant motion where specified; do not invent scoring windows or retime music. Use the actual frame/FPS/timebase and retain audio reference.

Keep background pulses subordinate and unrelated flashing out of timing lanes. Inspect near-hit/peak/release frames and full playback with visual guide markers. If glow merges consecutive events, reduce persistence/area or revise permitted staging. If a moving prop crosses a lane, relocate it instead of changing event timing. Deliver timed preview, hit-plane overlay and clean background layer; sync claims require decoded playback/audio verification, and input latency is untested.

## Educational assembly and demonstration

Identify the exact concept, age/context, source-backed relationships and requested labels. Build separable parts with stable pivots, assembly/exploded states and an explicit correspondence between diagram and physical model. Scale simplification can be useful, but record what was exaggerated or omitted. Avoid adding plausible-looking mechanisms or labels unsupported by the supplied evidence.

Use consistent color/material mapping across states and keep background quiet. Render assembled, exploded and partially restored views with callout-safe space; inspect hidden joints and occlusion. If parts seem interchangeable when they are not, strengthen keyed connection shape or explanatory layout. Deliver source/assumption ledger, state/pivot map and reviewed images. Clear assets do not demonstrate scientific accuracy beyond their sources or prove learning outcomes.
