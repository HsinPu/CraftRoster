# Strategy and Simulation Asset Kits: production playbook

## Brief, scale and evidence

Select RTS, turn-based, tower defense, MOBA, city/colony, farming or life simulation. Obtain zoom extremes, grid/hex and functional categories. Footprints and silhouettes do not prove pathfinding or economy.

## Asset kit and construction

Footprint planes, building/unit silhouettes, road connectors, boundary markers and subtype process props; label occupied, overhang and selection regions separately.

Use broad roofs and functional parts legible at far zoom. Reserve team zones and neutral variants so faction hue does not replace function. Document anchors, legal rotations and overhang exceptions.

## Bounded layout recipe

These dimensions are a starting study, not established game rules.

1. Place six configurable 4 m tiles or supplied hexes with path and three distinct functional building masses.
2. Render exact near/far cameras; repair primary silhouettes before increasing texture size.
3. RTS/turn-based: overlay faction/selection. Tower defense/MOBA: preserve lane and tower bases. City/colony: connect service fronts.
4. Farming/life: build plot/tool/storage and growth/use states; compare occupied versus decorative bounds and extended-state envelopes.

## Diagnostic images and acceptance

Footprint/overhang plan, far grayscale sheet, neutral/faction variants and connector seams. Measure tile fit and extended geometry envelopes.

## Specific failures and repairs

Tiny signs carry function: change roofs/functional masses. Canopy hides plots: group or lift foliage. Mill blade exceeds tile: declare overhang or revise geometry, without claiming navigation handles it.
