# Scale and unknowability

## Readable contradiction

Choose an ordinary witness and a vast unexplained form. A 1.8 m surveyor near a distant ring can communicate more than dozens of alien details. Decide what remains hidden: body extent, room connection or light source. Tentacles and gore are optional.

## Construction recipe

Block an observation platform and colossal curve disappearing beyond frame. Use intermediate stairs or repeated markers to bridge human and monumental scale. Add only a few asymmetric apertures to the remote form. Preserve large contour changes. For impossible architecture, align separate room sets to a fixed camera and plan an occluded transition; label it a shot illusion, not traversable non-Euclidean geometry.

Use broad mineral/unknown material regions. High-frequency bump can make a huge form read as a small rock, so scale texture against the witness. Let a grazing source reveal curvature and use weaker atmosphere for depth. Check the witness remains readable through fog. Compare compressed long-lens scale against a low wide vertigo view; choose deliberately.

## Diagnose and accept

Small-looking giants need texture rescaling and intermediate depth cues. Total incomprehensibility needs a mundane anchor. A familiar animal with extra limbs may need fewer descriptive details and stranger negative space.

Require intended/no-fog images, a silhouette and scale-context view. Accept believable magnitude, persuasive hidden extent and a legible premise. Moving spatial illusions require a viewed sequence and explicit camera/occlusion limits.
