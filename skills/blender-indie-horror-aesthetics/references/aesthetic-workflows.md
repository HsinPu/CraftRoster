# Independent horror aesthetic and location workflows

These are original production lanes informed by the upstream families. Named-game references help identify observable cues, not permission to copy assets, claim affiliation or reproduce gameplay. Choose one primary lane or explain a deliberate hybrid. Every branch produces an editable small set plus actual diagnostic images; no branch establishes AI, scares, level enjoyment or audio behavior.

## Domestic observation

Build a compact entry/living area using supplied household scale and time/place references. Door, seating, switch and window placement should explain ordinary use before introducing wrongness. Select one narrative displacement, such as a chair angled toward an empty doorway; unrelated ominous props dilute observation.

Use distinct ordinary materials: painted wall, fabric, laminate and glass should not all inherit the same rusty shader. Establish window/practical balance and a plausible carried-light direction when requested. Place an eye-height camera at entry and a second position approaching the displaced object. Keep any recording/UI treatment separate from the clean render and authored text supplied accurately.

If the set looks like a museum display, restore circulation and objects related to use. If it turns into retro slasher merely through posterization, restore the selected source fidelity. Review ordinary-use wide view, focal-object crop, clean/treated pair and approach images. Accept only when domestic function and the selected anomaly both read without invented background events.

## Angular retro/slasher staging

Build a roadside, motel or service threshold with modular walls and a readable silhouette prop. Allocate geometry/atlas detail according to the final low-resolution camera; the retro appearance does not mandate every upstream triangle number. Inspect large contour steps before microtexture. Separate intentional screen/texture limitations from VHS viewing-copy damage.

Give one practical a clear pool and preserve the intended threshold/route under reduced resolution. Use point-filtered texture tests where desired, with integer enlargement when the presentation calls for it. Keep violence at the approved intensity; it is not required to communicate this lane.

If outlines disappear, revise silhouette/texel coverage rather than adding random grain. If tape noise hides all staging, bypass it and restore the clean clue. Review base raster, atlas crop, effects-off view and a moving checker/actor sample if jitter or temporal distortion is claimed. This validates the authored treatment, not historical hardware behavior.

## Fog-bound institutional and corroded transition

Construct a repeatable street or institutional corridor from dimensioned bays, openings and recognizable services. Set a visible foreground destination and a farther continuation before fog. If the fiction includes an altered/corroded state, make it a matching variant with the same structural/pivot contract; show where intact paint becomes exposed substrate and where impossible alterations are deliberate.

Keep corrosion masks tied to coating failure, moisture and exposure. Avoid uniform red-brown metal on glass, plaster and tissue. Bound atmosphere to the shot and tune it against actual distances. Light a recognizable local anchor while leaving the chosen extent uncertain.

If atmosphere hides broken foreground joins, repair those joins in a fog-off view first. If the altered state is just a color grade, add a specific structural/material transformation justified by the brief. Review intact/altered comparison, fog-off/on pairs, corridor section and material transition crop. Do not claim that fog or geometry recreates a named game's sound or mechanics.

## Corporate emptiness

Define a believable floor system: ceiling tile/light grid, cubicle module, carpet circulation and conference-room boundary. Build one office bay and repeat it to test consistent fixture spacing and usable access. Then introduce a deliberate mismatch in occupancy or instruction, such as a fully prepared meeting room with an unexplained single disconnected desk. Use original or supplied signage and branding.

Separate fabric partitions, laminated surfaces, metal frames and fluorescent diffusers. Working, evenly repeated practicals can express a running institution without people; darkness is optional. Test a corridor-height camera and a conference-room reverse view so the space survives beyond a single crop.

If it reads as abandoned ruin, remove unsupported destruction and restore maintained systems. If it reads as generic yellow Backrooms, restore office-specific construction and task furniture. Accept with grid/plan view, module seam crop, signage legibility and intended image in which function and mismatch are both clear.

## Cabin and forest approaches

A cabin study needs timber construction, door/window thickness, heating/service clues and a deliberate interior/exterior relationship. Build one room and a woods-facing opening; retain reverse-side joinery instead of treating the wall as a painted plane. Materials follow grain, handling and weather exposure. Contrast a practical interior with exterior darkness or daylight according to the brief, not automatically warm-versus-blue.

A forest study needs terrain grade, path/ground layer, tree-trunk masses and limited unique landmarks. Place the approach camera before understory scatter. Ensure roots meet the ground, the trail edge remains readable and the cabin/formation outline is recognizable from selected turns. Fog and flashlight can restrict perception, but a brighter diagnostic must still explain the route.

If every tree is identical or the path is an opaque leaf wall, group depth layers and clear specific sightlines rather than deleting the entire understory. Review cabin inside/outside pair, construction crop, forest approach sequence and fog-off terrain view. These are visual route studies, not navigation or ecological tests.

## Hospital or clinical institution

Choose the actual institution and era; medical equipment must not be invented as an authoritative reconstruction. Build door/threshold, hard floor transitions, a privacy partition and one supplied clinical prop or service fixture. Scale clearance from project references, not a horror-specific corridor dimension imposed as regulation.

Separate cleanable floor, painted metal, fabric curtains and plastic equipment. Use plausible maintenance or targeted neglect based on the story; disease and trauma are not presumed merely because a medical object appears. Motivate overhead and task light. Inspect reflective flooring without turning it into a perfect mirror.

Generic factory read needs clinical construction and service context, not more blood. Curtain tangles need support/track and cloth-shape correction. Review threshold/prop scale, curtain/bed clearance, material crop and eye-height shot. Asset accuracy does not establish medical device function or safe facility design.

## Urban decay

Build a facade/alley or transit edge with masonry/concrete boundaries, drainage, repair patches and surviving access. Establish substrate and service paths before graffiti or litter. Orient runoff vertically and accumulate debris in protected/low areas; recent repairs should differ from older exposure.

Use a landmark opening and one local practical or sky direction so the route reads through layered urban density. Preserve a clean material/lighting comparison before creative grade. If every surface is uniformly dirty, divide exposure, handling and maintenance states. If the set becomes apocalypse ruins without authorization, restore intact construction and ordinary use.

Review drainage/repair crop, eye-height approach, roofless access plan and neutral/final pair. Accept recognizable urban construction, causal wear and a legible chosen destination. A Blender alley does not prove collision, social history or a functioning urban simulation.

## Hybrid and delivery check

For a hybrid, list which system each influence controls: domestic construction with a retro raster, for example, differs from randomly combining every iconic prop. Deliver source, texture/material dependencies, selected lane and exceptions, clean and treated renders, camera/output settings and image-specific findings. Recheck after changing the current revision; no written lane matrix is itself a visual pass.
