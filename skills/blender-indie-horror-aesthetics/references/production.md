# Reference families and places

## Extract visible cues

Identify camera height, shape density, texture treatment, light motivation, location and one narrative contrast. Separate these from gameplay and sound this package does not implement. Choose a primary reference lane rather than promising an exact named-game clone.

## Recipe matrix

- Domestic observational: plausible furnishings, ordinary room proportions, one misplaced household object and lamp/window relationships. Inspect without recording effects first.
- Retro slasher: angular silhouettes, small atlases and controlled low-resolution output. Build the motel or roadside threshold before tape damage; injury remains an explicit choice.
- Fog-bound institutional: establish corridor/street rhythm, then obscure distant continuation. Separate corrosion from intact paint. Foreground geometry must work without fog.
- Corporate: align ceiling, cubicle and carpet grids, then introduce one mismatch in access, signage or occupancy. Functional fluorescents can strengthen a system-without-people premise.
- Rural/cabin, hospital and urban decay: use location-specific evidence. Timber joins and a woods-facing window differ from floor transitions/privacy curtains or urban runoff/repair patches.

Build a single approach or room, focal prop and transition before a whole location. Choose texture/triangle budgets from camera coverage: a 128-pixel prop may suit retro work but damage contemporary domestic fidelity. Keep material finish and light distribution within the chosen lane. Composite only supported artifacts with bypass available.

## Repair and accept

A five-game collage needs incompatible cues removed. A generic location needs construction vocabulary before logos or posters. Review clean/treated images, a location-material crop and an intended-camera thumbnail. Accept a readable primary lane and place, approved intensity and a rationale for any hybrid. References do not establish affiliation or asset rights.
