# Puzzle and platforming construction branches

## Puzzle causality and reset

Use the supplied interaction graph and states. Build switch, conduit/connection, receiver and gate with stable pivots/anchors through initial, actuated, completed and reset states. If the connection is intentionally hidden, record that design requirement rather than invent a visible wire. Keep decorative machinery from sharing the same active signifier unless it truly belongs to the puzzle.

Use shape/position/value as well as approved color. Compare all states from the actual camera with emission off and at minimum size. If cause/effect cannot be followed, repair connector alignment/state hierarchy instead of adding unrelated blinking. If reset leaves a moved prop or closed surface mismatched to its proxy, fix the asset state. Deliver state-transition art sheet and actual geometry/anchor measurements; solvability and interaction logic require the game.

## Side-view platformer

Obtain the real jump/landing envelope and camera plane. Build three representative landing surfaces, one hazard and a moving platform, aligning visible tops with supplied contact proxies. Model edge thickness and background depth so decoration does not create false usable ledges. Test the highest/lowest supplied actor poses and platform start/middle/end, with the swept volume visible only in diagnostics.

Keep silhouettes of safe landings distinct from hazards and background. If a decorative lip appears landable, alter its slope/profile or approved signifier rather than silently changing collision. If the moving platform clips set dressing, relocate dressing and inspect both extremes. Deliver dimensioned side elevation, state/motion contact sheet and depth-revealing alternate view; geometric plausibility is not a controller jump test.

## Three-dimensional platformer

Use actual perspective camera/offset and movement envelope. Build landing pads with visible depth, approach/exit views and a destination landmark; inspect oblique near-edge views because plan spacing alone hides depth ambiguity. Moving platforms need full rotation/translation envelopes and stable visual attachment points. Camera collision geometry must remain separate from visible art.

Use surface value/edge/normal cues rather than color-only landing guidance. If a safe pad merges with background at a grazing view, repair backdrop separation or pad profile. If perspective foreshortening makes a gap misleading, report the art issue and adjust authorized layout/camera without changing movement rules. Deliver plan/section, approach/orbit samples and actual pad dimensions. Reachability, camera behavior and perceived fairness remain engine/playtest questions.

## Combined puzzle-platformer and deliberate illusion

Build a state table pairing each puzzle state with landing/contact geometry. A moving gate or activated bridge must not change apparent safety without the supplied signifier. Test both solved and reset traversal views, including object backs/undersides. If an optical illusion is intentional, preserve its exact camera/projection and also provide a diagnostic revealing the construction.

Repair accidental state/landing contradictions locally; do not erase a requested illusion by applying generic realism. Deliver state-specific surfaces/anchors, illusion camera settings and before/after views. Neither a forced-perspective screenshot nor a state graph proves that the puzzle is implemented or solvable.
