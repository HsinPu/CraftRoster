# Puzzle and Platformer Asset Clarity: production playbook

## Brief, scale and evidence

Obtain actual actor, jump-envelope, platform travel and puzzle-state specifications. Unknown dimensions remain proposed studies. Choose camera plane; a plausible render cannot establish reachability or solvability.

## Asset kit and construction

Safe floor, hazard edge, moving platform, switch, receiver, gate states, connection indicators and diagnostic trajectory/travel volumes.

Align visible landing tops with their intended proxy footprint. Identify ornamental overhangs explicitly. Use shape and value as well as color for hazards; preserve component identity across states.

## Bounded layout recipe

These dimensions are a starting study, not established game rules.

1. Place three landings at supplied distances on a configurable 0.5 m grid; add labeled trajectory and landing proxies.
2. Build one switch/receiver relationship using conduit or matching connectors; capture initial, actuated and reset states.
3. Place moving-platform start/end transforms and swept volume; inspect extremes and route clearance.
4. Dress safe/unsafe forms distinctly; render intended view and a depth-revealing perspective to expose false landings.

## Diagnostic images and acceptance

Dimensioned side elevation, untextured landing silhouettes, state contact sheet, motion extremes and grayscale hazard/connection preview. Inspect backs and undersides for misleading usable surfaces.

## Specific failures and repairs

Decorative lip looks landable: reshape or annotate intended footprint. Hue-only switch link: add keyed geometry or visible connection. Dressing intersects swept volume: relocate it instead of silently changing movement.
