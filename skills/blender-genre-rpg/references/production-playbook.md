# RPG Region and Equipment Kits: production playbook

## Brief, scale and evidence

Choose JRPG, CRPG, action RPG or MMO and obtain camera, actor/mount scale and service/loot vocabulary. Preserve user-authored world culture. Visual gear tiers are not item statistics and hub renders do not validate quests.

## Asset kit and construction

Hub entrance, service-door/stall families, region landmark, ordinary/rare container states, class equipment and scale actors.

Anchor regions in authored construction/material history. Separate quality from rarity; low-tier equipment need not be unusable. Preserve shared grip/sheath/attachment landmarks across gear tiers and check clearances before ornament.

## Bounded layout recipe

These dimensions are a starting study, not established game rules.

1. Block a configurable 20 m square on a 2 m grid with three supplied services and a landmark.
2. CRPG: inspect top-facing prop density. JRPG: stage theatrical arrival. ARPG: reserve floor for actor/loot overlaps.
3. Make three equipment tiers with compatible attachment landmarks; compare minimum screen size and non-color rarity cues if specified.
4. For MMO framing add specified mount/group proxies and render a busy hub study; describe visual capacity only, not network/crowd performance.

## Diagnostic images and acceptance

Hub approach/plan, service-symbol sheet, gear attachment details and worst-case loot cluster with effects off. Neutral lighting separates region/rarity identity from presentation.

## Specific failures and repairs

Every shop equal: establish entrance hierarchy. Rare loot resembles decoration: revise container form and placement. Upgraded gear clips wrists: repair proportions/attachments without changing actor scale or mechanics.
