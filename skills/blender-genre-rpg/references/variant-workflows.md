# RPG subtype production workflows

## Shared region, equipment and rarity contract

Collect camera/zoom, actor/mount scale, region culture and actual service/item vocabulary. Build a neutral material/state sheet before rarity effects. Quality, rarity, class and faction are distinct axes: a rare item need not be larger or more ornate unless approved. Preserve grip/sheath/attachment landmarks through equipment variants. Give interactive containers closed/open/depleted states with stable pivots and shape cues; do not make every decorated chest appear lootable.

## JRPG theatrical regions and arrivals

Choose the specific project staging rather than treating all JRPGs as one visual style. Build an arrival route with a landmark, foreground frame, inhabited service edge and a quieter departure. Use a repeated architectural motif and local material history to establish region identity. A 20 m hub study can reserve one theatrical reveal and two service approaches; keep actual actor-camera scale separate from monumental background exaggeration.

Use broad palette/value families with controlled accents at entrances and role cues. Test arrival, dialogue/party occupancy and reverse-departure cameras. If every shop competes with the landmark, reduce secondary height/contrast. If distant spectacle collapses at eye level, add human-scale threshold inserts and believable daily props. Deliver arrival sequence contact sheet, region motif/material sheet and service fronts. Quest flow, narrative pacing and emotional impact are not proven by the render.

## CRPG high-camera interaction density

Use the actual high-perspective/orthographic view and zoom extremes. Build a room/hub slice with interactable, decorative and inaccessible prop classes, named interaction anchors and roof/wall cutaways as supplied. Top-facing forms matter: handles only visible from floor eye may not communicate function. Stage a busy table/storage zone and a sparse route to test density rather than filling all surfaces equally.

Reserve team/selection effects from material identity and ensure props differ through silhouette/value. If small keys/books vanish, enlarge the signifier within approved dimensions, alter staging or provide the requested marker; do not invent a new UI. If roof/canopy obscures interactions, supply an approved cutaway state. Deliver interaction/state inventory, near/far top views and cutaway comparisons; systemic interactions, camera controls and puzzle solutions remain target work.

## ARPG combat, loot and effect overlap

Build a floor-dominant arena/path slice with actual actor reach/effect proxies and a worst-case loot cluster. Keep container and item-display geometry separate from drop/effect placeholders. Create equipment tiers sharing anchors and test a maximum-size weapon/costume against the supplied body. Limit decorative floor cracks/runes that resemble hazards or loot highlights.

Use quiet broad ground values, readable enemy/actor silhouettes and controlled rarity accents with non-color shape cues where specified. Test effects-off and overlap views at minimum screen size. If loot lights merge into a single carpet, reduce glow footprint and differentiate item/container mass before changing item statistics. If upgraded gear clips wrists, fix attachment/proportions. Deliver loot/state/gear sheet and actual camera images; drop rates, hit registration and encounter balance are unverified.

## MMORPG mounted scale and crowded hubs

Obtain mount/group dimensions, camera distance, service density and regional landmark plan. Build a hub slice with recognizable entrance, gathering area, service fronts and a large-scale route. Stage the largest mount and several supplied player-size proxies, including overhanging gear, rather than extrapolating from one standing mannequin. Keep shared equipment mounts and entrances consistent through faction/quality variants.

Use landmarks legible from approach and keep local service signs subordinate but readable at interaction distance. If mounts hide service doors, repair queue/staging/architecture within the supplied contract; do not claim crowd navigation solves it. If identity depends only on player-name UI, improve region/service silhouettes. Deliver mount/foot-scale sections, crowd-proxy renders and LOD/screen-size comparisons when requested. Proxy density is a visual study, not network, draw-call or population capacity proof.
