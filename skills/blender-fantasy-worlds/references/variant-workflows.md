# Fantasy construction branches

## Medieval-inspired low magic

Choose a specific local construction vocabulary from approved references; do not make dirt, European timber or a particular religion compulsory. Build one inhabited bay with load-bearing posts/wall core, roof support, doorway, drainage and a repair patch. Repeat it through an inside corner and roof junction. For a proposed two-meter module, preserve wall/roof connection planes while letting hand-crafted edges vary inside those bounds. Add a functional daily-life station such as storage, cooking or repair with plausible hand access and tool scale.

Use timber grain along members, stone/plaster as separate layers and iron fittings where mechanically meaningful. Metal straps should wrap or fasten parts rather than float on them. Light primarily through the opening and task practical; check neutral material response before warm grade. Inspect joinery cutaway, doorstep/handle height and roof seam. Uniform grime needs exposure/use masks; miniature-looking buildings need wall/beam and prop scale correction. Deliver modular connections and one repaired/intact pair; historical accuracy requires its own sources, and fantasy does not establish it.

## High fantasy and magical infrastructure

First define how magic changes construction, water, transport or illumination. Model source → conduit → receiver → service access and an inactive state. A crystal-powered bridge needs anchored terminals, a coherent span/field vocabulary and a route visible when emission is disabled; crystals scattered on generic masonry do not establish infrastructure. Scale a monumental portal around a smaller actual human doorway if both ceremony and habitation matter. Repeat heraldic motifs through structural silhouettes before surface engravings.

Keep stone, metal and emissive energy physically distinguishable. Limit luminous accents to functional nodes and preserve ordinary reflection/roughness so the inactive scene reads. Stage a heroic wide view, an arrival view and a maintenance close-up. If bloom erases heraldry, reduce emission/glare and improve source geometry. If a floating span appears accidentally unsupported, author a visible magical support rule or change the construction. Deliver active/inactive assemblies, service-chain diagram and connection/clearance notes; shader effects and bridge gameplay need separate target implementation.

## Dark fantasy, corruption and ruined grandeur

Specify prior purpose, damage cause and elapsed state before adding thorns/ash. Build an intact structural bay, create a recoverable damaged derivative and place rubble consistent with the breach. Corruption should follow an authored cause: roots pry joints, mineral growth follows seepage or supernatural tissue connects to a source. Vary the mechanism across primary/secondary scale instead of wrapping every surface in identical spikes. Retain human-scale stairs/handles against monumental masses.

Use broad value separation between actors, floor and background; dark fantasy need not be featureless black or compulsory gore. Separate ash, rust, wet stone and organic growth masks. A low key and sparse practicals can express mood, but maintain a brighter diagnostic and corruption-off view. Repair collapsed silhouettes through mass/lighting, not only outlines. Inspect supported broken slabs, doorway gauge and readable threat-free route markers. Deliver intact/damaged/corrupted states and their chronology; atmosphere alone cannot prove narrative or encounter quality.

## Urban fantasy: concealed and public magic

Retain the established city's building date, utilities and ordinary movement first. Add a hidden magical layer at service thresholds, basement access or selected signage; public magic instead needs visible distribution, maintenance and everyday adaptation. Build one modern frontage, ordinary entrance, magical threshold and interior/back-side service junction. Show who can see/use the threshold through supplied art rules rather than inventing access logic.

Contrast modern finish and old relic craft while keeping contact, scale and mounting plausible. A sigil projected on a wall, engraved into it and built as luminous tubing need different geometry/material treatment. Compare daylight ordinary-use, night magical and emission-off views from the same camera. If neon overwhelms city identity, reduce its area and retain ordinary window/street-light hierarchy. If hidden access cannot be found under the approved visibility rules, repair its authored signifier, not the game rules. Deliver mundane/magical variants and threshold states; urban lore, concealment logic and engine transitions remain explicit handoffs.
