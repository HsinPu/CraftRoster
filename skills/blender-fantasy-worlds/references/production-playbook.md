# Fantasy Worlds: production playbook

## Brief, scale and evidence

Select low-magic, high, dark or urban fantasy and define effects on labor, structure and transport. Medieval-inspired work need not use one geography or culture. Label historical evidence, user invention and inference separately.

## Asset kit and construction

Structural bay, entrance, roof junction, landmark, domestic props, scale inhabitant and magical-service component where needed. Include a repair/construction state.

Separate structure, ornament and supernatural suspension. Share stone/timber/metal/textile families with purposeful variation. Magic needs source, conduit, receiver and inactive state; keep an emission-off diagnostic to reveal form.

## Bounded layout recipe

These dimensions are a starting study, not established game rules.

1. Block a configurable 16 m courtyard on a 2 m grid with entrance, destination and inhabited edges before adding runes.
2. Repeat one structural bay to test roof, corner and doorway joins; define where craft or magic changes its assembly.
3. Connect water, lighting or transport through readable architecture and props; show magical service active and inactive.
4. Dress one daily-life zone at human scale, compare the distant landmark and save neutral/narrative-light views at matched transforms.

## Diagnostic images and acceptance

Eye view, roofless structure plan, seam sheet and magic-off comparison. Inspect stairs/doors with human proxy and landmark hierarchy in grayscale.

## Specific failures and repairs

Featureless dark palette: restore broad value groups and directional light. Generic crystal clutter: reduce sources and reveal their service chain. Unexplained floating masonry: provide authored magical support or repair structure. Anachronistic utility: explain or replace within scope.
