# Biome Worlds: production playbook

## Brief, scale and evidence

Choose terrestrial ecology, speculative biology or deliberate fantasy. Record substrate, water, elevation, season and recent disturbance. Placement follows these relationships rather than a biome-colored filter. Obtain camera range and inhabited routes before scatter.

## Asset kit and construction

Terrain patch, transition edge, geological masses, canopy/understory/ground layers, landmark organism or formation, water/ice where relevant, and human-scale proxy.

Use drainage, slope and exposure masks to direct ground cover. Make roots contact terrain and preserve editable water intersections. Review roughness under neutral light before atmosphere; fog must not conceal unbuilt geometry.

## Bounded layout recipe

These dimensions are a starting study, not established game rules.

1. Block a configurable 24 m patch on a 2 m grid with high point, channel and basin. Add two fixed eye cameras.
2. Place rock and canopy masses first; scatter by moisture and slope, preserving the review route and landmark silhouette.
3. Build a 6 m transition strip with interleaved species or material states tied to altitude, water or an authored fictional boundary.
4. Add climate lighting and atmosphere separately; capture identical atmosphere-off and atmosphere-on views plus a distant silhouette.

## Diagnostic images and acceptance

Ecological-zone plan, terrain section, fog-off eye view and far silhouette. Inspect roots, water seams, snow accumulation and island undersides in actual images.

## Specific failures and repairs

Bright leaf noise: group canopy values and reduce microcontrast. Hidden underwater subject: revise scattering distance/composition. Snow on sheltered undersides: fix accumulation masks. Floating roots: repair terrain contact before increasing density.
