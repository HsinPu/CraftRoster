# Medium-specific production workflows

Use the approved composition and medium. These branches are alternatives, not layers to stack by default. Keep the clean geometry, local-color textures and paper/canvas treatments separate. A painting-like raster does not establish physical pigment simulation, and a texture filter does not create intentional brushwork.

## Watercolor: paper, wash and edge flow

Start with a small still life or facade whose largest light, middle and dark shapes already read without texture. In a watercolor interpretation, untouched paper is a positive design region: save a light-region mask for highlights, sky gaps and selected silhouettes before adding color. Do not blanket-multiply a photograph of paper across every region and call the result a wash.

Author broad color washes using UV-aligned painted maps, projected illustration layers or an intentionally camera-specific compositor mask. Choose surface-attached marks for moving assets; a static plate may use screen-space marks if that limitation is declared. Separate wash opacity from pigment color. Overlap two or three purposeful washes so glazing changes color locally while retained paper lights remain clear. Sample a reference for chroma rather than forcing every watercolor to be desaturated.

Create edge-accumulation masks from the authored wash boundaries, not every geometric edge. A distance-to-mask-boundary band can seed darker pigment edges; break and vary it where the brush lifts. A backrun is an irregular advancing wash edge within a wet region, not a universal black outline around objects. Add one controlled backrun or bloom-shaped pigment displacement and compare its scale to the image. Preserve selected soft/lost edges away from focus while keeping one focal boundary crisp.

Use restrained nonmetallic surface response and broad illumination for the geometry contribution. Paper grain belongs at the chosen output scale; decide whether it modulates wash coverage or appears as an image substrate. Avoid large normal/displacement noise that makes a delicate wash look carved. Save wash-only, paper-only, overlay-off and final plates at the same camera and exposure.

Diagnose muddy color by bypassing dark-edge, paper and grade layers in turn. Restore reserved light regions before increasing saturation. Uniform dark rims need narrower, broken masks or fewer affected washes. A noisy photocopy read needs larger wash groups and reduced grain contrast. Accept only after inspecting actual-size paper lights, a focal edge crop, broad wash transparency and a final thumbnail. For motion, watch marks on turning surfaces to locate swimming; a still cannot approve temporal attachment.

## Oil: opaque masses, glaze and selective impasto

Block a value painting on simplified forms. Choose a stroke field that describes the subject: follow a jug's turning volume, cloth fold direction or the planar facets of a face. Keep stroke clusters larger than texture pores. Use painted vector/direction maps or deliberately aligned UV regions to orient marks; random isotropic noise cannot substitute for directional brush decisions.

Author large opaque color masses first. Add warm/cool shifts within those masses without dissolving their value hierarchy. Transparent-looking shadow glazes are a separate color-layer choice, while opaque light strokes may cross earlier boundaries. Preserve source layers if the painting tool supports them; a flattened export is a derivative rather than proof that layers exist.

For impasto, use a selective stroke-height map with a documented neutral value and limited amplitude. Test shading normal/bump first; true displacement needs enough geometry and close-camera evidence to justify its cost. Broad raised strokes may catch highlights; do not make every brush mark glossy plastic. Let canvas grain remain subordinate and consistent with apparent canvas/output scale. Compare a side-lit crop to an unbumped version so the surface relief supports rather than replaces painted form.

Light the source object with a broad key that reinforces painted masses and control specular separately. A fixed painting may carry designed highlights; a relightable game asset needs reduced baked illumination and an alternate-light check. Do not promise both without checking the contradiction. Record whether the output is an illustration or a physically relightable material.

If oil looks like embossed noise, reduce the number of stroke frequencies and align the remaining marks. If darks appear pasted flat, adjust glaze/color relationships rather than increasing physical roughness indiscriminately. If light rotates against painted highlights, choose and document the intended lighting contract. Accept with color/value study, stroke-direction crop, raking-light impasto comparison and final image; inspect a moving-light or camera test only when that behavior is part of delivery.

## Gouache or opaque illustration: matte coverage and dry edges

Use this branch only when the reference calls for it; it is an optional neighboring medium rather than an upstream requirement. Establish opaque flat regions with limited value steps and selective overlap. Create an edge mask for dry-brush breakup, leaving the interior coverage solid enough to read. Concentrate broken marks at chosen edges and transitions instead of roughening every fill.

Use matte nonmetallic response, retain visible construction planes where helpful and avoid watercolor-style transparent stacking in every shadow. Save a clean fill plate and a dry-edge plate. If the result resembles watercolor despite the brief, restore opacity and simplify layering; if it resembles vector art, introduce selective brush-shaped edge variation and value transitions without global grain. Review flat-fill, dry-edge and final crops at delivery scale.

## Medium handoff

Deliver editable geometry/materials, painted source or flattened derivatives labeled accurately, substrate/mask assets, camera/color settings and reviewed diagnostic plates. State which marks are UV/object-attached and which are screen-space. Baked painted shading fixes part of the illumination and is not an automatic portable dynamic shader. Renderer, target import and actual visual acceptance need their own evidence.
