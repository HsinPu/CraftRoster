# Choose a retrofuture lane

## Separate the vocabularies

Synthwave/outrun can use a dark road, graphic sunset and sharp emissive rhythm. Vaporwave may use pastel architectural fragments, nostalgic consumer objects and softer collage. These are possible references rather than interchangeable mandatory motifs. Select supplied cues and preserve any branding. Do not add arbitrary foreign-language signage without accurate supplied text.

## Scene recipe

For an outrun study construct a road or grid receding toward a focal silhouette, with one dominant horizon feature. Build neon strips as actual slender geometry with enough thickness to survive final pixels. Put grid spacing in world units and test near/far aliasing. For vaporwave replace the dark road grammar with a clean pastel arrangement and one historical/digital juxtaposition rather than simply changing the hue.

Use reflective metal or coated surfaces according to material: chrome needs metallic response and a readable reflected environment, while neon tubes need emission and adjacent illumination where supported. Test emission exposure before glow. Preserve bright cores and let halos remain weaker; a compositor glare pass should not become the only source of form. Compare roughness near 0.15 and 0.35 on reflective props to choose the right reflection width. Keep a non-emissive fill or reflection card so black objects do not disappear.

## Repair and acceptance

White neon blobs need lower exposure/emission or smaller halo. Invisible chrome needs environment/reflection structure, not brighter albedo. A generic purple fog scene needs lane-specific geometry and hierarchy restored.

Require glow-off/final images, a reflective-material crop and a final-size grid/line crop. Accept the selected lane, readable emissive cores, stable line rhythm and reflective object identity. Motion requires a viewed clip for grid shimmer; target real-time bloom requires its own renderer test.
