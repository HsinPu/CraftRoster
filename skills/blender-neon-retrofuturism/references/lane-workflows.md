# Retrofuture lane workflows

Use the main recipe for neon/reflection diagnostics. These branches differ in composition and object relationships, not merely hue. Preserve supplied brands/text; language or classical imagery is optional and requires appropriate source assets.

## Outrun or synthwave road study

Block a road/grid, a horizon silhouette and one foreground object at the chosen perspective. Establish grid spacing in world units, then project it through the final camera to inspect near/far frequency. Put the brightest rhythm along the composition rather than outlining every edge. Keep mountains or a sunset disc as a separate shape layer so hierarchy can be tuned without changing the road.

Build strips/tubes thick enough to survive the smallest output. Use emission for cores and supported spill lights where needed; a compositor halo does not light adjacent chrome. Give reflective objects an environment with broad readable bands and black separation. Compare glow-off/on, roughness alternatives and the darkest object against its ground.

If the grid aliases or shimmers, change projected spacing/line coverage and inspect motion before more blur. If all neon clips white, lower emission/exposure or halo strength while retaining color. Review final-size line crop, chrome reflection crop and full composition. Animated travel needs viewed motion; a still cannot establish stable raster behavior.

## Vaporwave collage or pastel architecture

Choose one spatial contradiction: a consumer-era screen in a classical court, a displaced palm within a retail interior, or a clean digital object among architectural fragments. Use rights-cleared or original geometry. Block three depth layers with deliberate negative space; a mandatory purple road would change this lane's composition rather than improve it.

Build plausible column/plinth/casing construction before introducing collage. Use broad pastel material groups and a clear light source; choose matte stone versus glossy plastic deliberately. Keep any nostalgic raster insert on a separate material/plate, including its actual screen border and pixel treatment. Restrict glitch or tape damage to the layer or viewing-copy stage the fiction supports.

Frame from the intended print/screen composition, then inspect a clean ungraded view. If the image is merely an empty pink room, strengthen the selected object/context relationship. If it turns into dark synthwave, revise light/background/material hierarchy rather than only changing the palette. If captions are unreadable or inaccurate, use the supplied text or remove them; arbitrary foreign lettering is not a required authenticity cue.

Accept with a three-layer blocking view, clean/treated pair, material comparison and final thumbnail showing the juxtaposition. Record whether the output is a fixed collage or a spatial asset. A perspective trick or screen-space overlay must not be promised as coherent from arbitrary viewpoints.
