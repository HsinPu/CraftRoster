# Tension through sightlines

## Choose the fear mechanism

State what the viewer anticipates and what is withheld: presence beyond a corner, a familiar room arranged incorrectly or an unsafe-looking route. Record setting, time and intensity. Fog, decay, historical technology and injury are optional; contemporary domestic and bright outdoor settings remain valid.

## Compact scene recipe

Block entrance, transition and obscured destination. Start from a dimensioned door and movement needs; a 1.4 m passage can express compression while a wider hall exposes the observer. These are shot experiments, not building regulations. Place one silhouette clue at the destination and a foreground interruption. One consequential blind corner is more useful than arbitrary turns everywhere.

Build meaningful ordinary props: a separated handset, misplaced chair or strained latch. Wear follows history. Motivate the focal pool with a fixture, window or carried source. Reduce fill to establish uncertainty, then lift selectively until route and clue remain identifiable. Capture key and fill separately because light-power ratios do not predict pixel contrast. Use atmosphere only when it improves depth.

## Diagnose and judge

Unreadable darkness is a visibility defect: repair composition, value separation or fill. A scary-prop catalogue needs fewer competing clues. Restore intensity if unrequested gore appeared.

Inspect neutral layout, intended view, grayscale thumbnail and brighter diagnostic image. Accept when both destination and uncertainty read and ordinary use can be inferred. Gameplay navigation, audio and triggers require separate implementation.
