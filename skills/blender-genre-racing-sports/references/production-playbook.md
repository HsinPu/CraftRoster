# Racing and Sports Environment Kits: production playbook

## Brief, scale and evidence

Select racing, field/court or arena sport. Obtain project dimensions, participant scale and camera. Source real rules when required; fictional sports may use authored geometry. Asset inspection does not validate handling, balance or rule compliance.

## Asset kit and construction

Racing: road, curbs, barrier, runoff, direction marker and landmark. Sports: boundary, scoring structure, stand bay, access tunnel and team props. Add dimensioned vehicle/athlete proxies.

Join roads with shared centerline tangent and cross-section, preserving banking and crown. Document curb/barrier connection frames. Keep sponsors separate from critical markings. Measure evaluated repetition without inferring runtime gains from instances.

## Bounded layout recipe

These dimensions are a starting study, not established game rules.

1. Build a configurable 60 m approach/bend/exit or representative stadium corner from approved dimensions.
2. Track: set driver cameras at turn-in, apex and exit. Sports: set athlete-eye and broadcast views including far framing.
3. Place an anticipatory landmark and clear runoff/edge form; keep sponsors away from scoring and boundary marks.
4. Prepare requested near/far variants and inspect fixed-size seam, sign and participant comparisons.

## Diagnostic images and acceptance

Centerline/cross-section overlays, curb close-up, three approach frames and far-detail sheet. Review sharp frames before optional blur; blur does not prove readability at a stated real speed.

## Specific failures and repairs

Curb reads as road: change profile/value groups. Barrier crosses runoff: repair placement/connection. Team identity conflicts with marks: separate shape and placement conventions. Banking seam: match complete cross-sections.
