# Wear with a cause

## Choose the history

A maintained oily machine, rain-soaked station and dry warehouse require different wear. Mark handling areas, water routes and exposed metal before adding dirt. Grit does not imply blood, monsters or total destruction.

## Production study

Build a service alcove with a substantial machine and accessible path. Give panels thickness; reserve silhouette damage for consequential breaks. Separate masks for coating loss, corrosion and dirt. Contacts remove paint, runoff stains vertically, oil gathers around mechanisms. Mix a metal substrate and dielectric coating rather than making every dark region metallic.

Compare rough concrete around 0.8 with a local oil film near 0.2, then tune from reference. Establish stains visible at the final distance before tiny scratches. Light the focal mechanism with a plausible practical and weaker sky/room fill. Cold surroundings with a small warmer source may express loneliness, but preserve a different established palette. Grade after inspecting an ungraded image and grayscale thumbnail; dark hierarchy must not erase the access path.

## Repairs and evidence

Uniform edge rust reads as a filter: break it by exposure and handling. Glossy dark dirt may indicate color and roughness were coupled incorrectly; inspect maps separately. An illegible machine needs selective fill or subject/background value separation before global exposure.

Require a neutral material crop, ungraded image, final grade and intended-distance view. Accept causal wear placement, distinct material regions, legible silhouette and retained focal detail. A weathered appearance does not establish a corrosion simulation.
