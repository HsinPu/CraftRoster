# Pastoral ritual

## Establish the fiction

Specify community, season and approved recurring symbol. For a real tradition use supplied or verified references rather than inventing sacred meanings. Rural life itself need not be dirty or threatening. Injury is a separate intensity choice.

## Scene recipe

Build a field edge, path and modest gathering structure with an ordinary gate as scale anchor. Align handmade offerings toward an absent center or converge paths on a single object. Construct a woven hero patch with simpler distant wicker; carved wood needs grain direction, tool cuts and joined parts rather than random erosion. Repeat one symbol family with slight handmade variation.

Test straw/wood around roughness 0.7–0.9 and a smoother handled object as focal contrast, then tune to the reference. Broad daylight or low afternoon sun reveals pastoral normality against the deliberate alignment. Compose along a visible path with ordinary landscape still present. Keep the grade grounded before adding a seasonal tint.

## Repair and judge

If it reads as a festival, adjust an empty center, blocked route or unnatural repetition before adding injury. If it reads as generic occult decoration, reduce symbol variety and make assembly/use plausible. Simplify foliage that obscures the arrangement.

Inspect wide context, an elevated arrangement view and a close assembly image. Accept when normality and anomaly both read, handmade joins hold at delivery distance and unresearched cultural practices are not presented as fact. This produces Blender scenes, not gameplay rituals.
