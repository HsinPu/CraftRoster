# Collapse, occupation and civic-state branches

## Post-apocalyptic wasteland

Declare event, time since collapse, climate and current repair capacity. Build a thick intact facade/vehicle/shelter master, then separate event damage, long-term weathering and recent salvage. A 12 m street slice can have one intact anchor, one breached bay and one inhabited repair. Orient rubble/scorch to the event and dust to wind/shelter; later tracks cut through older deposits. Tie tarp corners to actual anchors and add supported patched panels.

Use bleached sun-exposed paint, protected original color, material-specific corrosion and rough dust. Low hard light reveals damage depth, while neutral fill confirms interiors. If every substrate rusts, separate metal from concrete/glass/cloth masks. If habitation reads abandoned, add recent reachable tools, water access and repaired paths. Deliver chronology layers and clear-corridor/fog-off images; collapse art is not structural or survival-system validation.

## Zombie city and human response

Build ordinary urban access first: storefront, stairs, windows and a rear escape. Add barricades from reachable salvage with meaningful braces, fastening and human movement clearances. Stage intact, freshly blocked and breached variants sharing pivots. Broken glass, dragged furniture and discarded supplies should describe direction of pressure and occupation, not random gore. Keep quiet safe-space signifiers distinct from environmental damage if supplied by the project.

Separate old city weathering from fresh impacts, stains or makeshift repairs. Inspect entry/reverse views and crouched/standing proxies with effects off. A barricade that blocks all circulation needs redesigned placement, not silently shrunken actors. Clutter resembling pickups needs a distinct packaging/state grammar. Deliver door/window/barricade state sheet and assembly notes; zombie detection, AI routes and safe zones are not established by renders.

## Alien invasion and unfamiliar damage

Define one coherent foreign fabrication/impact vocabulary and how it differs from local buildings. Construct an ordinary bay, a contact zone and a foreign insert or landing footprint with consistent connector scale. Impact deformation must have a direction, thickness and debris logic; an alien graft needs roots/anchors and service/growth paths. Keep human reference elements intact nearby so unfamiliar scale is legible.

Use restrained foreign material response or a distinctive form hierarchy rather than making everything emissive. Render foreign layer off/on and a clean cross-section. If alien damage is indistinguishable from generic explosion rubble, revise characteristic cut/penetration/attachment shapes. If scale is unclear, add doors/vehicles with known size rather than labeling the object giant. Deliver source/contact/reaction layers and diagnostic close-ups; physical impact or fictional technology is unverified beyond the stated visual rule.

## Post-human ecological succession

Set climate and elapsed time, then stage building failure, water paths and successive plant colonization. Begin with roof/drain breakage and soil pockets; place small growth there before large roots/trees. Substantial plants need plausible support volumes or explicit fantasy. Build an early/late pair from the same architecture with preserved source and cameras, changing masks/distribution rather than merely increasing a scatter count.

Use moisture stains and protected versus exposed surfaces to connect vegetation to water. Show interiors and sheltered undersides, not only a green facade. If mature trees grow on unsupported glass, revise substrate/support. If lush growth contradicts a dry brief, adapt species/coverage and preserve the approved timeline. Deliver architecture/growth separately, succession masks and dry/wet contact views; these are authored visual states, not ecological simulation proof.

## Dystopian control infrastructure

Use only the control/oppression story actually requested. Build barriers, observation positions, checkpoint hardware and permitted/service circulation around a normal civic block. Give cameras/gates real mounting, cable/power routes and maintenance access; separate active/inactive/breached states. Distinguish permanent authority construction from improvised public responses through assembly, scale and repair quality.

Reserve graphic symbols/emission for selected control nodes. Frame both institutional wide symmetry and street-eye passage; inspect neutral lighting so intimidating grade does not replace actual spatial hierarchy. If all entrances look sealed but the brief needs habitation, add readable daily/service access. Deliver access-state sheet and hardware diagrams. Static sight cones or camera props do not prove surveillance, detection or authorization logic.

## Utopian civic systems and requested uncanny contrast

Clean, functional civic design can be sincere. Build maintained transit/water/garden/repair systems, accessible entrances and human-scale public use before optional uncanny staging. Create a service-open variant showing how immaculate surfaces are cleaned and repaired. If the brief asks for an uneasy veneer, introduce controlled repetition, selectively absent use traces or a precise compositional anomaly rather than automatically adding propaganda.

Use clean but varied roughness, realistic seams and restrained accents; avoid clipping white surfaces into featureless emission. Compare occupied/empty and neutral/atmospheric views. A sterile dollhouse needs contact, wear-at-use and service-scale correction. Deliver maintained/service/contrast states with exact intentional anomalies; no moral narrative or social outcome is inferred solely from the palette.
