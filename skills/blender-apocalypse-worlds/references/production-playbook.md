# Apocalypse and Dystopia Worlds: production playbook

## Brief, scale and evidence

Define collapse cause, elapsed time, surviving population and maintenance capacity. Distinguish original construction, event damage and later habitation. Clean civic design can be sincere; do not infer oppression unless the brief establishes it.

## Asset kit and construction

Intact/breached facade pair, thick floor slab, rubble cluster, barricade, repair patch, utility cabinet and inhabited shelter. Keep human and salvage-cart scale proxies.

Use identical pivots and boundary planes for intact/damaged variants. Separate fracture interiors, oxidation and recent repair masks. Model supported broken edges and meaningful wall thickness; scatter separate rubble instances rather than destroying the editable facade.

## Bounded layout recipe

These dimensions are a starting study, not established game rules.

1. Assemble a configurable 12 m street on a 2 m module with intact, damaged and repaired frontages; reserve a clear central corridor.
2. Orient debris and scorching from a declared event origin. Check slab support and reverse-side breach thickness before dressing.
3. Add later human responses in order: salvaged barrier, tied tarp, water store and a fresh path through older dust.
4. Create a contrasting repaired or controlled district state with the same cameras. Express access policy through actual barriers and entrances, not an unrequested political story.

## Diagnostic images and acceptance

Damage-layer exploded view, roofless plan, street-eye corridor and clean/weathered material sheet. Hide rubble and fog: remaining architecture must still explain event and passage.

## Specific failures and repairs

Uniform rust: remove it from protected interiors and distinguish glass, concrete and metal. Blocked corridor: move rubble and repeat eye view. Floating slab: repair support or document explicit supernatural fiction. Conflicting regrowth and occupation: resolve the timeline.
