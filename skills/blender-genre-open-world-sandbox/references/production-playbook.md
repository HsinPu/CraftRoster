# Open World and Sandbox Asset Layouts: production playbook

## Brief, scale and evidence

Define bounded study area, travel scale, farthest camera and exploration versus construction emphasis. Obtain chunk size, origin and assembly contract. A large Blender scene is not a tested streaming or playable world.

## Asset kit and construction

Terrain chunks, border transitions, regional landmark, subordinate POIs, path cues and construction pieces when requested. Separate bounds, silhouettes and interaction proxies from scatter.

Keep chunk-relative pivots and shared edge coordinates. Use nested silhouette scales instead of identical beacons. Scatter by authored density zones; retain boundary inspection without atmosphere. Construction kits need attachment faces and legal rotations.

## Bounded layout recipe

These dimensions are a starting study, not established game rules.

1. Assemble a configurable four-chunk 128 m study on a 32 m grid with high/low ground and three approach cameras.
2. Place a regional silhouette and two subordinate POIs with different outlines; prevent competing focal peaks.
3. Blend biome strips through terrain-related material/species changes and path crumbs rather than uniform signposts.
4. For construction work, assemble/disassemble a shelter and show attachment planes. Capture boundary sweeps before final scatter.

## Diagnostic images and acceptance

Three approach renders, boundary sections, grayscale distance tiers, origin/bounds overlay and scatter-off terrain. Compare LOD silhouette at matched screen size when requested; counts do not imply frame rate.

## Specific failures and repairs

Hidden landmark: revise canopy or approach silhouette. Cracked terrain: reconcile shared edge coordinates and normals. Identical POIs: differentiate roof/terrain forms rather than saturating more beacons.
