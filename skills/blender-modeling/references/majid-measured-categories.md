# Measured construction by asset family

Choose a construction for each part from its references, motion and export needs. This adapts Majid's category recipes; helper names describe techniques, not installed functions. Preserve a complete editable source and treat familiar-object dimensions as estimates until confirmed. The workflow can be performed through supported bpy/data operations or an authorized live tool.

| Part and measured inputs | Construction sequence | Failure check before detail |
|---|---|---|
| Organic torso/limb: joint centers and sectional radii | Connect a joint graph; skin/subdivide a blockout, or fuse measured masses on a separate high copy; build deliberate low joint loops afterward | Branches pinching, missing mass, accidental mirrored joints, motion range; a uniform remesh is not deformation topology |
| Vehicle/hull: side height and top half-width at stations | Use 6–12 useful section stations as a starting example; order equal-point rings consistently, bridge, cap as needed; add support/bevels at actual panel breaks | Twisted ring correspondence, changing winding, uneven highlights, silhouette between stations; more stations only where curvature requires them |
| Wheel/column/vase: radial profile and axis | Spin a measured profile, merge only the intended full-turn seam, cap exposed ends; orient to the real axle or architectural axis | Duplicate revolution seam, inward caps, wrong pivot; a 360-degree operation can leave coincident surfaces |
| Arch/blade/beam: closed planar outline and depth | Extrude a validated outline; preserve wall/blade thickness, openings and functional grip | Self-intersecting profile, zero thickness, reversed cap and insufficient edge width at gameplay size |
| Tail/cable/horn/rail: centerline and section/taper | Sample a curve, choose stable local frame and taper, create a tube or supported curve source; convert only the owned delivery copy when needed | Sharp kinks, frame flips, excessive bevel subdivisions, unsupported curve export |
| Cloth/panel/membrane: surface boundary and overlap order | Build a grid with rows supporting actual folds/bends, add measured thickness after deformation where appropriate | Accidental armpit bridge, membrane inversion, doubled deformation and insufficient clearance |
| Repeated windows/steps/treads | Define one owned module and array/instance along explicit spacing or path; count evaluated/realized delivery geometry | Curve orientation/stretch, overlap at closure, unsupported instances and doubled endpoint |

Mirror about a declared symmetry plane, often a shared centerline empty rather than each displaced object's origin. Preserve approved asymmetry on a separate stage. Use boolean cutters on an editable copy, inspect near-coplanar slivers and grazing highlights, then evaluate the intended delivery result. A successful modifier does not prove clean surface or useful topology.

## Creature and moving-part branches

For a quadruped, label scapula/shoulder/elbow/carpus and hip/stifle/hock before modeling; the backward-looking lower limb is not a reversed knee. Build separate ribcage/pelvis and a spine/neck/tail graph. Place jaw/teeth/eyes/claws separately when they move or shade independently. For a wing, choose bird feathers versus digit-supported membrane, add rows along both relevant fold directions, then test fold/spread/downstroke with rough deformation. Do not infer flight competence from a wingspan ratio.

For a serpentine or multi-limbed creature, record section taper, ground contacts and each limb's functional range. Check the most compressed and extended pose before scales/spines. Use geometry where outline or articulation requires it; bake small surface breakup from a dense source when the target budget requires.

## Modular architecture: a concrete assembly gate

For a requested 2 m kit, define shared cell, wall/floor thickness, door opening and connection points once. Produce straight, corner, end, doorway/window and roof pieces with floor/grid-corner pivots. Make a 2×2 assembly containing a corner, doorway and adjacent floor; inspect inside and outside from eye/gameplay height. Check seam world coordinates, overlapping coplanar faces, z-fighting and trim continuity. Test the declared player/door clearance proxy through the opening; a rendered opening alone does not prove navigation.

Doors/shutters/gates remain independent with hinges on the actual hinge line. Colliders are simple owned proxies per intended physical region; ornament count does not dictate physics density. Set tile UV scale from actual brick/plank dimensions, not a unique large texture per repeated stone. A hero building may combine modules and unique pieces when the brief needs them; modular construction is not mandatory for every architectural asset.

## Vehicle and weapon branches

Record length/width/height, wheelbase/track, wheel diameter, ground clearance, section stations and visible interior depth. Separate steering pivot from spin axle, doors from body, and glass/lights from paint. Tracked vehicles need a documented loop/spacing and test at closure; animated tracks need an actual target-supported representation. Aircraft control surfaces and rotors, boat draft and waterline, and hover clearance use different references and acceptance; a model is not proof of drivability/flight/buoyancy.

A held weapon's pivot/grip frame must match the consumer's hand attachment convention. Keep triggers/bolts/lids and bowstrings separate when they move. Test the actual hand/attachment through the relevant pose and check muzzle/tip direction against intended effect/hit direction. A decorative socket empty does not demonstrate a usable grip.

## Environment and cleanup branches

Rocks/cliffs can use measured masses and controlled reduction; preserve a flat/buried placement base and silhouette at each LOD. Trees need separate trunk/canopy/wind representations as required; leaf-card cutout, mip coverage and overdraw need target inspection. Terrain chunks need shared boundary samples to tile without cracks. Instance a small test patch at intended gameplay density before multiplying variation; a single attractive piece cannot establish repetition quality.

Before accepting any branch, inspect evaluated counts and finite coordinates, degenerate triangles, intended boundaries and material assignments. Keep base topology checks distinct: loose vertices, near-duplicates, winding and islands need scale-aware tolerances and semantic exceptions (an eye or leaf card can legitimately be detached/open). Do not weld intentional UV/hard-normal splits indiscriminately. Add a target-specific collider closure and influence check only to the appropriate semantic subset. Preserve source, dimensions, part map and current diagnostic images; do not label unexecuted checks passed.
