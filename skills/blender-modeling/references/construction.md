# Construction branches

## Hard surfaces and props
Use real joins, hinges and assembly boundaries to organize parts; stylized construction can deliberately depart from manufacturing realism. Test cuts on a copy, evaluate bevel width at physical scale, and inspect grazing-light highlights. Choose edge normals and modifier behavior supported by the actual Blender version. Do not blindly use removed Auto Smooth settings or apply every modifier.

## Characters and creatures
Use a proportion blockout and the motion brief to place joints and major masses. Preserve character identity in a flat silhouette. Sculpting can supply a high-detail source; build deformation-appropriate delivery topology afterward. Triangles and poles are judged by deformation and shading, not banned everywhere. Clothes, armor and detachable parts follow the intended interchange and motion contract.

## Modular environments and procedural assets
Define the grid and connection points from the project's level design. Test a corner, straight segment and junction together at gameplay scale. Reuse instances where supported and count realized geometry before export. Seed variation; keep density and detail ranges bounded. Distinguish source-only nodes, evaluated meshes and engine-supported instances.

## Geometry versus texture
Keep silhouette-changing parts and functional moving pieces in geometry. Surface marks may be baked or painted. Never remove hidden body regions until all supported equipment and poses have been checked. Preserve the full master and make coverage cuts only on a delivery copy.
