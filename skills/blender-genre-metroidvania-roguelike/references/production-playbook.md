# Metroidvania and Roguelike Room Kits: production playbook

## Brief, scale and evidence

Separate persistent interconnected exploration from recombinable runs. Obtain movement dimensions, camera plane and assembly rules. Mesh joins can be checked here; progression and procedural solvability need game implementation.

## Asset kit and construction

Wall/floor/corner modules, socket gauges, ability gate, upgrade landmark, ordinary/elite marker and secret-wall variant. Keep modifier signs detachable.

Specify socket origin, normal, opening dimensions, floor height and clearance. Preserve boundary planes across variants. Give gates locked, open and inactive visual states without implying the logic is implemented.

## Bounded layout recipe

These dimensions are a starting study, not established game rules.

1. Make entry, vertical connector and landmark room footprints on a configurable 2 m grid with named socket empties and clearance boxes.
2. For persistent exploration, stage a visible return loop and future destination beyond a gate; label the access diagram as proposed.
3. For run recombination, join and rotate the rooms in two explicit arrangements; inspect normals, floor continuity and arrival views.
4. Capture gate/elite/secret states before dressing; retain critical identity through shape and value as well as hue.

## Diagnostic images and acceptance

Socket cross-sections, both arrangements from arrival cameras, vertical gate elevation and untextured room contact sheet. Measure gaps/floor offsets and compare secret cues against ordinary damage.

## Specific failures and repairs

Opening faces solid wall: fix socket normal or compatibility label. Every crack implies secret: reserve a consistent construction cue. Elite marker resembles ambient light: give a physical frame and stable silhouette.
