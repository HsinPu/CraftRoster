# Narrative and Visual Novel Sets: production playbook

## Brief, scale and evidence

Record story beat, viewpoint, blocking and output: VN plate, interactive film or walking scene. Collect approved dialogue masks and composition. Emotional framing is an art goal, not proof of audience response.

## Asset kit and construction

Editable set, story prop, foreground framing, actor stand-ins, fixed cameras, time/state variants and requested plate/depth outputs.

Separate depth layers and use overlay masks instead of visible text-safe geometry. Reuse transforms/layout across time variants; isolate intentional story-state changes. Keep depth of field reversible to reveal continuity errors.

## Bounded layout recipe

These dimensions are a starting study, not established game rules.

1. Stage a configurable 6 by 8 m room with door, window, story prop and two actors; apply the approved dialogue mask.
2. VN: test seated/standing/close-up actor occupancy. Film: block reverse angles and sight direction. Walking scene: inspect entry and object-eye views.
3. Create morning/evening lighting without moving furniture; name intentional clue states and their shot assignments.
4. Render clean plate, actor-overlaid review and agreed layers. Preserve ungraded output and inspect final crop/dialogue safety.

## Diagnostic images and acceptance

Matched day/night images, reverse-angle continuity, actor/dialogue overlays and focus-off views. Check reflections for missing subjects or production geometry. Static plate approval does not prove walkable collision.

## Specific failures and repairs

Shelf crosses head: revise owned framing within approved camera scope. Evening moves clue: restore common layout and isolate intentional state. Blur hides story object: repair focus/hierarchy instead of declaring it readable.
