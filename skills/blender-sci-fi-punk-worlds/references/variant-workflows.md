# Science-fiction and punk construction branches

Select a lane or declare which subsystems use each lane in a hybrid. The dimensions below are configurable studies. Scientific-looking detail is not engineering or safety validation.

## Hard science fiction

Write gravity, pressure, crew/service scale, power and thermal assumptions. Build a habitat/service bay around a human proxy: pressure boundary, access hatch, replaceable panel, connector and equipment withdrawal envelope. Separate structural frame from covers. Route cables/pipes between actual terminals, place fasteners where covers can open and show an exposed maintenance state. In zero-g fiction provide handholds and orientation cues rather than gratuitous floor clutter; in rotating/artificial gravity show its declared direction consistently.

Use subdued coatings, bare metal only where appropriate and small status indicators separated from structural emission. Neutral side light should expose panel depth and seals. If detail becomes meaningless greebles, remove elements without attachment/service purpose. If a hatch intersects its frame or equipment cannot be withdrawn, repair the mechanism envelope. Deliver pressure/structure/service layer diagram, closed/open states and crew-eye review. These are visual consistency checks, not pressure-vessel or radiation certification.

## Space opera

Establish symbolic scale and faction form language before microdetail. Block a monumental hangar/ship silhouette with a small repeated crew/access element to communicate size. Build primary keel/tower/engine masses, secondary bays and only then antennas, surface panels and hero insignia. Keep a service-scale interior insert separate from ceremonial exterior openings if their scales differ intentionally.

Use material/value groups to preserve silhouette at distant camera; emissive engines should not bleach hull panels. Compare hero wide, distant black silhouette and maintenance-eye detail. If scale shifts between shots, fix repeated windows/doors and detail frequency rather than claiming every tiny panel is huge. Deliver scale sheet and near/far variants, with fictional technology labeled. Dramatic scale does not justify accidental floating parts, source destruction or false engine-performance claims.

## Cyberpunk

Organize tower, street and service strata with deliberate access and infrastructure. A 12 m alley study can include occupied frontage, overhead utility rack, public sign, repair stall and a quieter destination. Model sign brackets, cable sag/endpoints, drainage and repair layers. Reserve pedestrian/camera clearance before adding hanging cables or umbrellas. Vertical density must retain readable hierarchy from street eye and an elevated view.

Use wet response by substrate, controlled luminous signage and ordinary shadow-side surfaces. Keep original sign faces/emissive materials independent so animation does not affect building walls. If neon alone supplies identity, strengthen stacked massing and service history. If reflections erase the route, reduce saturated area/roughness extremes and inspect dry/emission-off versions. Deliver dry/wet and sign active/inactive states; political narratives and city systems follow the supplied brief rather than the genre name.

## Steampunk

Define steam source, working machine, exhaust/condensate route and service access. Build boiler-like mass, pipes with coherent diameters/bends, valve/gauge station and driven mechanism as distinct assemblies. Use brackets and flange/fastener logic; do not attach every pipe to nowhere. Show one open maintenance cover and a stopped state. A decorative gauge is not a measured operating instrument.

Separate painted iron/steel structure from copper/brass fittings and worn hand-contact parts. Warm light can support the palette but inspect neutral metal response. If everything reads as gold plastic, correct conductor masks, roughness and reflections. If moving rods intersect housings, repair pivots/envelopes or label the mechanism static/decorative. Deliver source-to-work diagram, component pivots and stopped/working visual states; no pressure or machine feasibility claim.

## Dieselpunk

Choose a coherent retro-industrial reference vocabulary instead of combining unrelated military eras. Build a heavy powertrain housing, frame rails, vents, access doors and fuel/service elements with readable load-bearing versus replaceable parts. An oversized grille needs an actual behind-grille volume; exhaust and intake must be visually distinct. Keep a technician proxy and panel-removal space.

Use broad painted panels, exposed wear at edges/fasteners and material-specific oil/dust deposits. A hard oblique key reveals stamped/cast shape; avoid uniform black grime. If machinery looks like a riveted cube, add meaningful secondary volumes before bolt scatter. If an access hatch is hidden by a pipe, reroute within the declared subsystem. Deliver panel/structure exploded view and clean/used variants, noting all fictional mechanism assumptions.

## Atompunk

Select a particular retrofuture reference set: rounded enclosures, analog controls, exposed/chrome trim or monumental energy motifs are options, not mandatory historical facts. Build one domestic/public appliance and one infrastructure module sharing radii, control hierarchy and connector vocabulary. Keep operator reach, screen visibility and removable back panel explicit; ornamental atomic rings need clearance from actual handles.

Use enamel-like colored coating, limited chrome and readable dials instead of uniform mirror metal. Test warm interior and neutral studio views; large white highlights must not erase control markings. If the design reads only as pastel color, reinforce enclosure/profile and analog interface shape. Deliver front/back/service views and inactive/active displays. Treat period inspiration as a designed interpretation unless independently sourced.

## Solarpunk

Integrate power generation, water, shade, planting and maintenance rather than placing vines on a generic tower. Build a small civic module with solar/shade canopy, drainage/cistern, growing substrate and a repair path. Orient panels and plant zones according to the authored sun/site assumptions; leave service access around connectors and pruning areas. Show seasonal or maintained/overgrown variants when requested.

Use warm daylight, varied matte construction and limited reflective panel surfaces. Keep plant root volumes and watering routes visible in cutaway. Plants obscuring panels/doors need relocation or a documented functional tradeoff. If every surface is green, restore material hierarchy and inhabited wear. Deliver water/power/planting layers and maintained-state images; render plausibility does not prove energy output, irrigation performance or sustainability.

## Biopunk

Define whether the system is grown tissue, synthetic imitation or a hybrid. Model containment, nutrient/fluid interface, organic support and technician separation. A wet-lab module needs vessels with actual thickness, ports/tubes ending in appropriate couplings and access for replacement. A grown machine needs branching/attachment logic and inactive/dormant state, not random flesh noise over hard-surface geometry.

Separate dry enclosure, translucent membrane, wet tissue and fluid response. Controlled subsurface scale and a backlit diagnostic reveal thickness; avoid using emissive red to conceal missing form. If tubes appear fused without connection or tissue floats, repair interfaces. If subsurface washes out all detail, check units and scale first. Deliver containment/organic layers, exposed/sealed variants and explicit fictional biology; no medical, containment or laboratory safety claim.

## Clockpunk

Choose power input, gear train, escapement-like visual motif and work output within an authored pre-steam fiction. Build a small layered mechanism with gear planes, shafts, bearings, frame and removable cover. Record tooth counts and approximate ratios for coordinated animation; place meshing gears at appropriate center separation and different planes where needed. A gear engraved as relief is different from a moving gear and should be labeled.

Use limited brass/steel/wood choices, contact polish and cavity oil. An orthographic exploded view plus side section must reveal clearance between wheels/shafts/frame. If animated teeth intersect, correct ratio, phase, size or pivots before blurring motion. If tiny gears vanish at target size, simplify the train and preserve a few functional masses. Deliver static/decorative versus animated membership and pivot/ratio sheet; coordinated rotations alone do not prove mechanically valid tooth profiles or efficiency.
