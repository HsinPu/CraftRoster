# Science Fiction and Punk World Kits: production playbook

## Brief, scale and evidence

Choose a lane or intentional hybrid, energy source, fabrication and gravity/pressure fiction. Do not impose brass, neon or oppression universally. Separate scientific assumptions from style; functional-looking machinery is not engineering proof.

## Asset kit and construction

Structural bay, service panel, power/transport element, storage, threshold and technician proxy, with active/inactive and maintained/repaired states.

Separate structure, replaceable panels, connectors and service access. Relate detail scale to fabrication/users. Isolate indicators from structural emission. Show agreed seals/handles/orientation for pressure or zero-g fiction without asserting safety.

## Bounded layout recipe

These dimensions are a starting study, not established game rules.

1. Block a configurable 12 m service lane and machine bay on a 1 m grid with access-envelope proxy.
2. Trace source, conduit and terminal using consistent connector/panel logic.
3. Build maintenance states and opening study; assign hybrid technologies to explicit subsystems instead of random motifs.
4. Compare neutral/presentation and emission-off/on views; inspect access and fabrication hierarchy without glow or fog.

## Diagnostic images and acceptance

Exploded maintenance view, source-chain diagram, access section and emission pairs. Inspect panel backs/cable endpoints. Label static props versus proposed mechanisms.

## Specific failures and repairs

Neon-only identity: rebuild main masses/interface. Intersecting gears: revise clearance or label relief decorative. Plants block panels: preserve service route. Unrelated motifs: unify selected subsystem logic without forbidding intentional hybrids.
