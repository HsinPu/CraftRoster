# Monochrome panel design

## Define reading order

Set dimensions, reading direction and action. Use actual reference proportions rather than inferring anatomy from demographic labels. Establish black anchors, blank paper and tone mids before surface detail. Desaturating a color anime image at the end does not establish intentional print values.

## Panel construction

Block pose and background in grayscale with a few distinct large value regions. Protect a clean facial zone. Use hair, costume or cast shadow as an anchor; separate adjacent black regions with paper gaps or controlled lighter edges when necessary. Keep material highlights subordinate to drawn shape rather than realistic glossy noise.

Create line and fill plates separately. Hatching follows form; screentone covers selected midtones. Begin near a 5-pixel pattern repeat in a digital preview and revise against actual resolution and reproduction. Avoid stacking rotated screens unless interference is intended. Optional speed lines converge toward action and stop short of critical hands/face. Keep effects-free pose evidence.

## Repair and acceptance

Merged hair/coat/background needs negative-space and value grouping before thicker lines. Over-toned expressions need masks and reduced coverage. If speed lines provide all the energy, revise gesture and camera.

Inspect line-only, large-value-block, complete panel and actual-size tone crop. Accept clear reading order, expressive action, deliberate black/paper balance and stable tone. State whether review was on-screen or print-proofed; neither proves the other. Retain editable plates for later editorial use.
