# Soulslike Spatial Art Studies: production playbook

## Brief, scale and evidence

Define original tone and supplied actor/camera/checkpoint/shortcut vocabulary instead of copying a franchise. Monumentality and vulnerability guide art; challenge, loops and combat fairness require implementation.

## Asset kit and construction

Monumental bay, stair/elevator shell, return passage, original rest landmark, authored mist threshold and contrasting actors.

Balance large masses with human-scale steps/handles. Separate controllable fog from its readable frame. Keep actor/stone value separation and vertical diagrams outside export geometry. A rest marker need not be a bonfire.

## Bounded layout recipe

These dimensions are a starting study, not established game rules.

1. Block a configurable 24 m tall two-level ruin with visible lower arrival; set player-eye and monumental cameras.
2. Add stair/elevator shell reconnecting visually to the earlier space; record matching elevations, not an assumed operating lift.
3. Place rest marker and threshold with active/inactive variants and distinct light language.
4. Dress exposure-based decay and inspect return, entry and camera-edge actor views before tuning atmosphere.

## Diagnostic images and acceptance

Vertical section, upper/lower landmark view, fog-off/on threshold and grayscale actor sheet. Include stair-corner and elevator-opening clearance views.

## Specific failures and repairs

Oversized usable doors: separate ceremonial framing from human inserts. Grey stone swallows actor: revise broad values/light before outlines. Mist hides frame: adjust coverage. False lift landing: reconcile elevation and visible access.
