# Survival resource and shelter workflows

## Wilderness gathering

Choose habitat, season and actual resource vocabulary from the game. Build wood/stone/metal/food or supplied equivalents as source, collectible and depleted states with stable base/pivot and distinct packaging/profile. A harvested tree stump, mined rock or picked plant must retain identity without a permanent glow. Place resources through substrate/moisture/exposure constraints rather than uniform scatter; preserve the collection route/camera.

Use material-specific color/roughness and daylight/low-light comparisons. If pickups resemble inaccessible background, revise silhouette/state placement within the brief. If depletion changes collision-looking mass unexpectedly, identify the art/proxy discrepancy for handoff. Deliver resource-state lineup and habitat/contact views. Regrowth, gathering time, nutritional value and economy are unimplemented unless tested separately.

## Crafting bench and camp shelter

Use actual body/tool reach, workstation states and shelter access dimensions. Build worktop, storage, tool locations and worker slot as a use sequence, with input/output/repair states only where requested. Place roof/wall/sleep/storage interfaces around the actual opening and overhead clearance; separate removable panels and reversible construction stages.

A motivated task light can reveal the active bench while leaving camp quieter. Inspect worker-eye, side reach and shelter inside/outside; open doors/storage must not block worker space. If a tool cannot reach the work surface or a seated actor floats, repair owned geometry/anchors rather than inventing a new animation. Deliver workstation contact markers, build/use states and assembly/clearance sheet; crafting recipes, weather protection and navigation need actual systems evidence.

## Urban salvage and container language

Define material history and which objects are collectible, searchable, inaccessible or already depleted. Build a street/interior slice with one salvage source, storage and a clear path; keep ordinary decorative cabinets from sharing the complete searchable-container signifier. Create intact/open/stripped/repaired variants with consistent hinges/pivots and recognizable silhouettes.

Separate old exposure damage from fresh scavenging marks and current repair. If everything is uniformly rusty, restore protected paint, glass/concrete/cloth differences and localized handling. Inspect low-light search distance and maximum opened extents. Deliver container/state/access sheet and salvage chronology. A visually open crate does not establish loot contents or search interaction.

## Survival horror scarcity and return routes

Use horror only when requested, preserving the resource/interface vocabulary from the other branches. Build a bounded tense approach, remembered safe-space landmark and optional risk shortcut according to supplied route rules. Stage critical resources where the actual camera can distinguish them from debris under scarce practicals; darkness should not conceal missing construction or unreviewed collisions.

Compare ordinary/alert or supplied danger state, effects-off view and brighter diagnostic. If a safe landmark resembles a hazard, separate physical form/value before changing hue. If the only available pickup cue is bloom, improve source geometry/state contrast. Deliver route/landmark/state images and sampled clearances, with enemy behavior, true safety, scarcity balance and emotional response remaining unverified.
