# Survival and Crafting Asset Language: production playbook

## Brief, scale and evidence

Define setting, resource/safe-space vocabulary and whether horror is requested. Obtain actor scale and container/use states. Props communicate scarcity but do not validate crafting economy, survival balance or enemies.

## Asset kit and construction

Resource families, full/empty storage, workstation, shelter boundary, route landmark and depleted/repaired variants; distinguish pickups from inaccessible background.

Separate wood/stone/metal/food by silhouette and packaging, not only hue. Build bench reach/tool clearances. Tie aging to use/weather and distinguish fresh repairs from abandonment.

## Bounded layout recipe

These dimensions are a starting study, not established game rules.

1. Block a configurable 20 m camp edge with bench/storage and two approaches; set collection/entrance cameras.
2. Create world/collectible/depleted resource states where specified and compare daylight/low-light views.
3. Assemble bench and shelter with attachment and scale references; keep full/empty and repaired/intact pivots consistent.
4. Dress risk-route landmark and camp boundary; tune sparse motivated light and recheck pickups without glow.

## Diagnostic images and acceptance

Resource-state sheet, bench reach elevation, shelter inside/outside and day/night collection images. Neutral silhouettes expose clutter confusion. Route overlays describe intent rather than verified AI safety.

## Specific failures and repairs

Glow-only pickup: revise packaging/shape. Every storage shell looks lootable: separate inaccessible forms. Dark bench unreadable: add motivated task light. Unreachable worktop: repair dimensions without rewriting crafting rules.
