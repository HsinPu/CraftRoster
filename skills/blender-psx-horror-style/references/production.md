# Retro constraints with a fear mechanism

## Declare authenticity level

Choose evocative modern PSX style or specific historical constraints. State camera, triangle/texture budgets and one tension premise. Low-count geometry alone does not produce affine interpolation or screen-space vertex snapping. Document any approximation and target-renderer requirement. Tape damage is optional and separate from console raster behavior.

## Compact study

Build a motel threshold or institutional room with an ordinary scale cue and one distant uncertain shape. For a small background prop, 80–300 triangles and a 64–128-pixel texture can be an initial study; camera coverage and the brief override it. Use silhouette-efficient triangulation and controlled face normals. Allocate texels to recognizable labels/forms rather than unreadable tiny text. Keep a coherent low-color ramp and nearest texture interpolation where desired.

Use one plausible practical and weak ambient contribution to retain the clue. Fog may limit visible extent but is not mandatory; a clear black void or dry interior may fit better. Check the no-fog scene for broken foreground geometry. Compare a low-resolution image such as 320 by 240 with the requested final presentation using integer enlargement when appropriate. Model-space snapping is not equivalent to camera-dependent screen jitter; do not label it faithful hardware emulation. Avoid intentional z-fighting as a substitute for instability.

## Repairs and evidence

Modern shiny micro-PBR may need a simpler texture/value treatment. Excess VHS obscuring the clue needs removal/localization. Random wobble needs camera-space logic and a reference, not deformation of every mesh.

Require evaluated counts, texture crop, low-resolution final and effects-off view. For jitter/affine treatment inspect an actual moving checker test. Accept recognizable angular assets, purposeful texture crunch and readable dread; claim engine fidelity only after target verification.
