# Remembered place, displaced object

## Ground nostalgia

Choose a time/place vocabulary from supplied references: faded playground, old computer room or bright family corridor. Do not invent personal childhood or trauma. Select one displacement, such as carpet extending into a field, and distinguish wistful from threatening mood. Captions are optional authored graphics.

## Shot recipe

Model ordinary door, chair and ceiling proportions first. Repeat one remembered motif, then break its context. In a playground study place an oversized slide beside a normal bench; an empty landscape alone offers no nostalgic anchor. Use aged molded plastic and broad faded paint rather than universal rusty horror surfaces.

Start with plausible overcast or fluorescent lighting, then introduce one mismatch such as a window brighter than its apparent exterior. Render clean before treatment. Add gentle halation, low-resolution inserts or recording artifacts only when supported by the reference. At 1080-pixel output, compare a 1-pixel softness experiment with the clean plate before stacking blur. Put any caption on a separate layer and check its supplied wording.

## Repair and acceptance

Generic Backrooms needs the specific remembered object restored. Excess bloom needs recovered local edges. A threat collage needs unrequested eyes, wounds and ominous captions removed.

Review clean/processed images, an anchor-object crop and a thumbnail. Accept recognizable nostalgic vocabulary, an intentional displacement and the agreed emotion. A still does not validate temporal distortion; view the sequence when animated.
