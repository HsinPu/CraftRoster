# Stealth Environment Art Studies: production playbook

## Brief, scale and evidence

Obtain stance/eye dimensions, concealment classes and project definitions of visible/noisy surfaces. Darkness may be a fiction cue rather than a numeric rule. A rendered shadow cannot prove AI invisibility; a material cannot prove sound propagation.

## Asset kit and construction

Opaque cover, porous screen, practical fixture, vent/ledge kit, surface samples and route-state props. Keep labeled patrol/sight proxies out of exports.

Reconcile visible opacity/openings with intended proxy. Motivate light pools physically and retain legible boundaries. Distinguish grate, carpet, water and debris by geometry/roughness while leaving sound behavior to the game contract.

## Bounded layout recipe

These dimensions are a starting study, not established game rules.

1. Block a configurable 16 m court with lit crossing and sheltered perimeter; set supplied guard-eye and stance cameras.
2. Add vent/ledge entrance with opening gauge; inspect approach, entry and exit rather than assuming traversability.
3. Overlay selected static cones; test screens/foliage against intended concealment proxies.
4. Assign surface families to routes, review with overlays hidden, then render night and brighter diagnostic states.

## Diagnostic images and acceptance

Roofless route/cone diagram, crouched/standing views, opacity section and surface sheet. Raise exposure on sheltered zones to examine geometry/source logic; these remain static checks.

## Specific failures and repairs

Black grading hides edges: restore local separation. Hedge implies solid concealment but proxy is open: reconcile intent. Vent too small: enlarge or label decorative, never silently shrink supplied actor. Loud-looking surface: report art cue separately from audio behavior.
