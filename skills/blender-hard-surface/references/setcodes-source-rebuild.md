# Rebuild a generated rigid prop from measured surfaces

Import into an isolated owned scene and enumerate all imported meshes; do not select the first mesh and assume it is the whole asset. Record evaluated dimensions, source material roles and transform/unit conversion. Capture orthographic front/back/left/right/top and perspective low-angle views. Mark protruding silhouette features separately from painted surface marks. Windows, boards and small keys may bake; fascia, brackets, deep openings and overhang edges need geometry whenever their parallax or outline is visible.

Create a BVH from evaluated source geometry. Either cast in world coordinates against an explicitly world-transformed copy or convert ray origins by the inverse matrix and directions by its inverse linear part, then convert hits back. Do not assume imported transforms are identity. Sample roof profiles across width at several depths, front/back wall planes and porch/step heights. Rays for steps start below the overhang; a ray from above the roof only measures the canopy. Use a measured scale anchor; door height is an estimate unless supplied.

Build closed 2D XZ/YZ profiles and extrude along the remaining axis. For a building retain walls/gables, a roof thickness profile, and separate barge-board bands. Offset a trim segment by its perpendicular normal; intersect neighboring offset lines only if determinant exceeds a scale-aware tolerance. Near-parallel/zero-length segments require a capped bevel or merged segment, not division by zero. Extend barge boards back to the wall so no source underside peeks out. A pent-roof skirt needs fascia and a closed soffit. Build plinth top as a visible ring instead of a full hidden face. Drop contact/bottom faces only after all required angles establish invisibility; keep the complete master.

Join a triangulated delivery copy and detect coplanar problems in a shared plane basis. Cluster candidate normals/distances using scale-aware tolerances, then intersect projected triangles robustly. Positive overlap area with same-facing normals indicates potential z-fighting; opposite-facing contact is removable only when the intended surface is fully covered and closed-volume/shadow needs permit it. Seven barycentric samples cannot prove containment: either compute polygon union coverage or retain ambiguous faces and inspect. Never delete solely from rounded-normal buckets.

Before baking compare the rebuild and source with identical world-registered cameras. For hard discontinuities choose consistent triangulation, normal splits/UV seams and a scale-derived cage; a 0.03 scene-unit extrusion on a small Meshy building cannot be reused after a sevenfold scale change. Bake requested Base Color via the intended connected signal through emission, including metallic surfaces. Process all intended source material slots, restore original links in `finally`, and keep material sources intact. Match sources by part or explode pairs if roof/trim rays cross-hit.

If the underside has projection garbage, inspect the actual faces and rays first. Repair the geometry/cage when visible; only a confirmed low-priority rigid underside may use a bounded flatten/fill map candidate. Do not shrink a UV island whose bottom is visible in required views. Reinspect gables, eaves, porch undersides and grazing highlights; an isometric overview hides these failures. Deliver measured source/profile tables, clean source and low copies, bake settings/maps and current paired images.
