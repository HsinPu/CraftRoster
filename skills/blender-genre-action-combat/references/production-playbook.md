# Action and Combat Asset Staging: production playbook

## Brief, scale and evidence

Distinguish lateral fighter, depth-lane brawler and free-camera action/hack-and-slash. Obtain actor scale, motion envelopes and destructible rules. A swing proxy does not prove hit registration, combat balance or encounter quality.

## Asset kit and construction

Floor and perimeter modules, elevation transition, intact/broken props, actor mannequins and labeled sweep-envelope meshes in separate diagnostic collections.

Use quiet floor groups behind actors. Differentiate breakables by construction as well as color. Keep overhead obstructions adjustable and preserve break-state pivots/piece IDs. Broken fragments must be tested as visible extents too.

## Bounded layout recipe

These dimensions are a starting study, not established game rules.

1. Block a configurable 14 m arena with a central action zone and perimeter band on a 1 m grid; place opposing actors and supplied reach volumes.
2. Fighter: test lateral frame edges; brawler: test depth lanes and feet; free camera: sample orbits behind columns.
3. Place one elevation change and one breakable family outside initial sweep zones, then compare intact and broken silhouettes.
4. Add quiet floor and readable boundary materials; inspect attack extremes before adding particles or rubble detail.

## Diagnostic images and acceptance

Overhead envelopes, actor silhouettes at frame edges, cameras behind major obstructions and intact/broken contact sheet. Flat mannequins expose limb/floor merging.

## Specific failures and repairs

Pillar hides weapon: move obstruction or repair camera clearance; do not shorten supplied reach. Floor cracks resemble warnings: lower their contrast. Breakables resemble structure: change construction grammar and inspect post-break state.
