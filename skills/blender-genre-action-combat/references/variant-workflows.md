# Combat staging branches

Use supplied actor reach, camera and state rules. Swing/effect proxies are geometric observations only. Keep intact/broken structures and temporary action envelopes in separate collections.

## Lateral fighting game

Block the actual lateral action plane, camera range and stage boundaries before background dressing. Place two actors at center and extreme separation, including crouch/jump/long reach poses from the supplied animation envelope. Build the floor contact strip with a stable horizon and reserve quiet values behind heads/hands. Background pillars can be richly staged outside the playable plane but must not merge with weapon silhouettes at common positions.

Test camera center and both frame edges at minimum output size, with flat actors and effects off/on. If feet disappear into floor pattern, reduce pattern contrast and clarify contact shadows rather than changing actor height. If the widest pose crops, repair owned camera/stage framing within the agreed contract. Deliver floor/boundary modules, pose/frame-edge contact sheet and background depth diagram; this does not validate collision boxes, frame timing or competitive balance.

## Beat 'em up and depth-lane brawler

Build a shallow depth arena with multiple actor feet visible at near/far positions. Specify whether decorative steps are usable height or background only. Stage a door/arrival, one obstacle and a breakable family with consistent depth cues, keeping the action floor comparatively quiet. Use contact shadows and perspective scale to reveal depth without painting false lane boundaries unless requested.

Inspect overlapping actors, near-edge props and a knocked-down proxy from the actual camera. If a foreground barrel hides feet/hand contact, lower/move the obstruction or provide the approved transparency variant. Broken props must not become an unreadable rubble carpet. Deliver depth-position sheet, intact/broken states and arrival views; depth sorting, movement lanes and enemy spawns require runtime tests.

## Hack-and-slash and free-camera melee

Use full body, weapon and camera-orbit envelopes. Build a representative central zone, peripheral cover/columns and elevation transition, then place multiple actor/effect proxies at a congested point. Test camera behind every major obstruction and at walls; floors need enough material structure for depth but must not resemble warning telegraphs. Breakable states share pivots and preserve required route/arena extents.

If particles erase attack silhouette, change approved effect staging/contrast before adding more light. If a column blocks the swing view, repair obstruction/camera clearance rather than shortening supplied reach. Deliver overhead envelopes, orbit contact sheet and worst-case effects-off/on images. Mesh clearance cannot establish lock-on, hit registration or encounter quality.

## Action-adventure transitions

Connect action space to a noncombat interaction/traversal threshold with the supplied movement and camera requirements. Build the same doorway/ledge from both arrival directions, with inactive/active interaction states and consistent material cues. Keep narrative dressing away from the widest required pose and camera sweep.

Review quiet exploration, interaction and combat framing at the same junction. If a decorative ledge appears usable but is not, reconcile visible geometry with the supplied proxy or label it explicitly for handoff. Deliver transition-state and reverse-view sheet plus attachment/clearance notes; do not invent traversal abilities or camera behavior to justify the layout.
